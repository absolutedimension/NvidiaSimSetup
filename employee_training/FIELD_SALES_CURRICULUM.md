## 0. The goal we hold (Goal OS)

> **"In 3 weeks, Rohan can walk into any Patna institute, understand its real problem, demo Acharya
> confidently, handle any objection honestly, and move the lead to a live pilot — without guessing
> and without over-promising."**

Acharya holds this goal, points one focused step at it each day, and won't mark a concept "learned"
until Rohan can **explain it back + apply it to a scenario** (the mastery gate). Confident-but-wrong
answers are caught, not passed — the whole point for a fresher who *thinks* he knows the pitch.

**Graduation = "Certified to solo":** passes every module's mastery gate + completes one full Maya
owner role-play + has one real pilot live in the field.

---

## 1. How each concept is taught (the repeating shape)

For every concept below, the tutor runs the same loop:
1. **Teach** — the idea in 3–5 plain lines (English; Hindi where it's a line he'll actually say).
2. **Check understanding** — "explain it back in your own words."
3. **Apply** — a real Patna scenario question ("Owner says X — what do you do?").
4. **Catch confident-wrong** — the common fresher mistake is named and tested.
5. **Space it** — the concept comes back days later (SRS) so it sticks.

Each concept card carries: **Objective · Key content · Mastery check · The trap (confident-wrong) · Role-play tie-in.**

## MODULE 1 — Product Mastery (Acharya inside-out)
*Urgent first: he's in front of owners now and must stop guessing. ~6 concepts.*

**1.1 What Acharya is — in one line**
- *Objective:* say what Acharya is (and isn't) in one clean sentence.
- *Key:* an **authentic exam-paper & assessment engine + per-student weak-topic tracking**. NOT a tutor that replaces the teacher; NOT a course-selling platform.
- *Mastery:* explain it in one line; then explain what it is NOT.
- *Trap:* calling it "an AI tutor / ChatGPT for students" — that invites the "we already have ChatGPT" objection.
- *Role-play:* owner asks "yeh hai kya?" → 15-second answer.

**1.2 The verified question bank (the moat)**
- *Objective:* explain the 1.45-lakh number and what "verified" means.
- *Key:* **1,45,000+ real, verified questions** — actual past-paper/exemplar Qs, each **solved and cross-checked against the official answer key**, cleaned + de-duplicated.
- *Mastery:* explain why a DIY app can't match this quickly.
- *Trap:* saying "we generate questions with AI" without the *verified/real* part — that sounds like everyone else.
- *Role-play:* "aap questions kahan se laate ho?"

**1.3 Authentic exam pattern + coverage**
- *Objective:* name the exams live today and how questions match the real paper.
- *Key:* JEE Main, JEE Advanced (Phy/Chem/Maths), **NEET incl. Biology**, CBSE boards, UPSC, Banking. Tagged by **chapter · concept · difficulty**, generated in the **real exam's pattern**.
- *Mastery:* pick an exam the owner teaches → say what we cover for it.
- *Trap:* over-claiming an exam we don't cover well — check, don't guess.

**1.4 Per-student weak-topic tracking (the real value)**
- *Objective:* explain that Acharya tracks each student, not just makes a paper.
- *Key:* per-student **weak-topic detection, mastery, spaced revision**. The teacher sees *which student is weak in which chapter*.
- *Mastery:* explain the difference between "a test-maker" and "an assessment engine that tracks."
- *Trap:* demoing only paper-generation and forgetting the tracking — that's the differentiator.

**1.5 The product surfaces (who uses what)**
- *Objective:* map the four ways Acharya is used.
- *Key:* **Students** (`/exam-prep`) · **Teachers/Institutes** (`/teacher`: batch tests, class weak-spot dashboard, share link, students take with no signup) · **Acharya Kids** (`kids-education.trigunai.com`) · **WhatsApp** (practise in chat, no app).
- *Mastery:* for a Class 6–12 + JEE/NEET institute, which surfaces fit?
- *Trap:* confusing the student self-serve with the teacher/institute flow.

**1.6 Why we're genuinely better (vs DIY / ChatGPT)**
- *Objective:* deliver the "why us" answer with confidence.
- *Key:* real vs guessed questions · verified vs wrong answers · unlimited + copyright-clean · tracks each student · diagrams done right · instant at scale · bilingual. (= the `WHY_ACHARYA.pdf` points.)
- *Mastery:* give the 20-second "why not just use ChatGPT" answer.
- *Trap:* getting defensive/arguing — instead, *invite* them to test ChatGPT's answers.
- *Role-play:* "main apna app bana lunga / ChatGPT se kar lunga."

## MODULE 2 — Vision & How We Show Up
*So he represents the company with clarity, not a sales script. ~5 concepts.*

**2.1 Vision vs Mission (he must hold BOTH)** —
- **Vision (where we're going):** *agentic AI for education as a whole* — solving every real problem in how students learn (testing, attendance, fees, parent-updates, and more) with agentic AI. The big picture.
- **Mission (what we're doing NOW):** the **assessment / test-paper engine — Acharya**. This is the one concrete thing we're executing today; it's how we *start* delivering the vision, and our first proven step.
- *Mastery:* say the vision in one line AND the mission in one line, and explain how the mission serves the vision. *Trap:* pitching "a product" instead of "we solve your real problem" — or confusing the big vision with what we can honestly deliver today.

**2.2 The first-hypothesis framing** — the mission (test generation + tracking) is **hypothesis #1** we're testing on the way to the vision; open to attendance/fees/parent-updates next. *Mastery:* explain why we say "first hypothesis." *Trap:* claiming we already do everything the vision describes.

**2.3 The stance: research, not selling** — we go to *understand the problem*; a paying customer is **proof** we solved it, not a target we push. *Role-play:* "aap kyun aaye ho?" → the research answer.

**2.4 Who we are** — founders: **Deepak** (Co-Founder, CEO & CTO — leads agentic AI for education) + **Avinash** (Co-Founder — deep-tech research, building Pratima); small focused team; Rohan is the Patna point-of-contact. (= the founders kit.) *Trap:* "sleeping partner" / vague answers that read weak.

**2.5 The honesty rules** — never "thousands use it" / never guarantee marks / never take cash / "I'll confirm" when unsure. *Mastery:* explain *why* honesty wins the trust sale for a new company. *Trap:* inflating to sound bigger — it loses trust and the sale.

## MODULE 3 — The Field Playbook (the method)
*Turns visits into a repeatable system. ~6 concepts.*

**3.1 The 7-stage lead journey** — Sourced → Warmed → Met → Wowed → Pilot Live → Usage Proven → Paid; **every lead sits at a known stage**. *Mastery:* place 3 sample leads on the ladder + name the one action that advances each.

**3.2 Warm before you walk** — appointment-first; **timing science** (owners present in evening batch hours); **beachhead cluster** (own Kurji/Boring Rd); send the **self-demo link** ahead. *Trap:* cold-walking with no appointment and blaming "owner not there."

**3.3 The owner-absent protocol** — collect the intake sheet (`ROHAN_VISIT_QUESTIONS`), turn the gatekeeper into an ally, **book a specific return**, send the demo link. *Mastery:* list the 5 non-negotiables to leave with. *Role-play:* owner absent → what he says to the receptionist.

**3.4 Discovery-first** — open with *"abhi test papers kaise banate ho?"*; make the owner **name the pain** before any pitch. *Trap:* pitching before they've named a problem.

**3.5 Hand-them-the-phone demo** — owner types a doubt on *their* subject → then the **confidently-wrong catch** (the wow) → **leave it running = the pilot**. *Mastery:* run the 3-minute demo flow from memory. *Role-play:* full demo with Maya as the owner.

**3.6 The follow-up cadence** — nurture live pilots (*"aaj 12 students ne test diya"*); **the fortune is in the follow-up**; log every visit same-day. *Trap:* demoing then going silent for a week (lead goes cold).

## MODULE 4 — Objection Handling
*The moment that wins or loses the room. Each = objection + honest answer + drilled with Maya. ~6 concepts.*

- **4.1 "ChatGPT hai na / apna app bana lunga"** → invite them to test ChatGPT's answers; the verified bank is the moat.
- **4.2 "Bahut mehnga hai"** → value framing + **free to start, pay after month 1** (no risk).
- **4.3 "Students use nahi karenge"** → that's exactly what the free pilot proves; watch real usage.
- **4.4 "Main technical nahi hoon"** → we set it up; students just use WhatsApp.
- **4.5 "Aapke customer dikhao"** → honest: newly launched, **founding-partner** advantage.
- **4.6 "Sochta hoon / baad mein"** → surface the *real* reason; book a **specific** next step, not a vague "later."

*Mastery for each:* deliver the honest answer in ≤20 seconds without over-promising.
*Role-play:* Maya throws all six at him in one mock call.

---

## MODULE 5 — Sales Fundamentals for a Fresher
*The underlying craft. Ongoing, not one-time. ~6 concepts.*

- **5.1 Listen more than you talk** — the 70/30 rule; stay silent after you ask.
- **5.2 Ask, don't pitch** — question-led selling; "how do you do X now?" opens every door.
- **5.3 Read buying signals** — what a real yes / soft maybe / polite no looks like; **usage is the truest signal**.
- **5.4 Rapport & trust** — the teacher is the hero; Acharya (and Rohan) are the helpful assistant, never the star.
- **5.5 Follow-up discipline & logging** — the CRM habit; an unlogged lead is a lost lead.
- **5.6 Handling rejection** — a **"no + reason" is data**, not failure; protect energy, log the why, move on.

*Mastery:* for each, apply it to one of his real recent visits.

## 2. The 3-week pacing (WhatsApp daily drip)

| Week | Focus | Daily shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Module 1 (Product) + Module 2 (Vision) | AM: 1 concept + recall Q (5 min) · PM: quiz + spaced review (5–10 min) |
| **Week 2** | Module 3 (Field Playbook) + Module 4 (Objections) | same drip + **2 Maya role-plays** (owner-absent, then a demo) |
| **Week 3** | Module 5 (Fundamentals) + full mixed review | same drip + **1 full Maya objection gauntlet** + first pilot push |

**Daily rhythm (light — he's in the field):**
- **Morning (5 min):** Acharya sends today's concept + 1 recall question → read before visits.
- **Field:** apply it; log the visit.
- **Evening (5–10 min):** Acharya quizzes today's concept + spaced-reviews an older one.
- **Weekly:** one Maya voice role-play + Deepak glances at Rohan's mastery dashboard.
