## 2. The modern pipeline (2026) — the five stages
Every channel below maps to one stage. Don't confuse "I posted" (stage 1) with "someone paid" (stage 4).

`AWARENESS → CAPTURE (email) → NURTURE → CONVERT (live cohort) → RETAIN / REFER`

- **Awareness:** short-form video (the #1 organic lever in 2026), long-form YouTube, founder-led LinkedIn/X, SEO/AEO, communities.
- **Capture:** turn views into **emails** — free class waitlist, lead magnet, the LMS signup. *The email list is the compounding asset; track it as a core metric.*
- **Nurture:** email sequence + community + retargeting → warm the list with proof and story.
- **Convert:** a **free live intro class** → application → close on a 1:1 call → bank transfer. (Your cohort model = high-touch close, not a checkout button.)
- **Retain/Refer:** wow the first cohort → testimonials + referrals → the cheapest acquisition you'll ever get.

## 4. The PAID engine — sequence it, don't rush it
**GATE: spend ₹0 on ads until ≥3–5 people have paid from organic.** Paid amplifies a funnel that works; it cannot create one. Once proven, scale in this ROI order:

1. **Retargeting first (warmest, cheapest).** Pixel your site + YouTube viewers → show the cohort offer to people who already engaged. Highest conversion, lowest spend.
2. **YouTube ads** — run your *own episodes/reels* as ads to AI/dev interests + a lookalike of your students. Native, cheap in India, plays to your strength (video).
3. **Meta (Instagram/Facebook)** — lookalike audiences seeded from your 4 students; the reels as ad creative. Best for the ₹17,999 Factories (B persona, impulse-friendlier price).
4. **Google Search** — high-intent keywords ("learn AI agents course", "VR development course India"). Highest intent, but competitive/pricier — last, and only for the flagship cohorts.

**Budget discipline:** start ₹500–1,000/day on **retargeting only**, for 2 weeks, measuring cost-per-enrolment. Kill what doesn't convert; double what does. Never scale an unproven ad.

## 7. Metrics — track these, ignore vanity
- **North star (the gate):** paid enrolments. Until this moves, nothing else counts.
- **Leading indicators:** email-list size (the compounding asset), free signups, **intro-call → paid %**, short-form views → profile visits, reply/DM rate.
- **Once paid is live:** CAC (cost per paid student), LTV, organic vs paid mix.
- **The pirate funnel:** Acquisition → Activation (signup) → Retention (watches modules) → **Revenue (pays)** → Referral. Find the leakiest stage and fix that one.
