## 1. What business are we in (one line)
We sell **live, project-based AI cohorts where the NVIDIA GPU is provided** — taught by a founder
who has actually shipped — turning people who *understand* AI into people who can *build and ship* it.

## 2. The thesis / promise
**"Free to understand. Paid to transform."**
- Understanding is **free** (the YouTube series + recorded modules) → reach + trust + email list.
- Transformation is **paid** (the live cohort) → where the money and the moat are.

## 3. What we sell — the value ladder (the product line)
| Tier | Product | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | "AI is the Universal Mind" series + recorded modules (EN+HI) | ₹0 | Funnel + trust + list |
| Entry | AI Video Factory · AI Music Factory (15-day) | ₹17,999 | Low-friction first "yes" (Persona B) |
| **Core** | **Live cohorts** — Agentic · ML · VR/MR · VR Game · Blender+Unity Game · Physical AI | **₹35–49k** | **The profit engine** |
| Future | B2B AI-literacy, Course 5 (AI Video Studio) | later | Post-validation |

## 4. The SPEARHEAD — what we lead with (the one bet)
**"Build Agentic AI Systems" live cohort (₹35k).**
Why this one, not the 8 at once: it's where **real buyers already showed up** (3 of your 4 signups
chose it). We pour everything into making *one* cohort sell and succeed; the rest stay as
waitlist/funnel until Agentic is validated. *(Decision to confirm — see §8.)*

## 5. Why we win (the moat — uncopyable, repeat it everywhere)
> Provided **NVIDIA GPU** + **live, small-group** + a real **project you ship** + taught by a
> founder who **shipped a Meta Quest app and trained a drone policy in Isaac Sim.**
Generic Udemy/Coursera/YouTube **cannot** match provided-GPU + live + "I built it." That's the wedge.

## 6. Who we serve (1-line; detailed in Doc 3)
**Primary buyer:** the **Builder** (developer who wants to ship) + the **Future-Proofer** (early-career,
anxious about the AI economy). **Not** the free watcher — they fuel reach, they rarely pay.

## 7. The business model / how money flows
Free content → **email capture** → nurture → **free live intro class** → **1:1 call → bank transfer**
(application-based, small cohort). No gateway/checkout — high-touch close. The compounding assets are
the **email list** + the **evergreen content library**.

## 8. The 30-day goal + the gate (what success looks like by July 18)
- **North star:** **first paid cohort — 8–10 paying students** in Agentic (≈₹2.8–3.5L from one cohort you run yourself).
- **The gate (this week):** **first cleared payment.** 0 paid today; 4 warm leads (Kritansh, Gauri, Aditya → Agentic; Prince → ML, call today). Nothing counts until ₹1 clears.

## 9. What we deliberately DON'T do (strategic focus)
- ❌ Market all 8 courses at once — spearhead Agentic; rest = waitlist.
- ❌ Run paid ads before ≥3–5 organic paid (no proof = burning money).
- ❌ Sell "certification" as the value (Trigunaï certs carry no market weight yet — sell the *skill + shipped project*).
- ❌ Build more product/curriculum instead of selling.
- ❌ Pour effort into the 7-follower brand Page — sell from the founder's profile + 1:1.
