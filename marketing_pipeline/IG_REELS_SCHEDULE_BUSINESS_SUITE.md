**Account:** @trigunaiinnovations  ·  **Reels:** 12 ready on the Mac  ·  Updated 2026-07-02

> instead of dumping at once. Post time: **7–9 pm IST**.

---

## STEP 0 — one-time setup (do this first, ~5 min)

Business Suite scheduling **requires your Instagram connected to a Facebook Page.**

1. Make sure you have a **Facebook Page** for TrigunAI (create one if not: facebook.com → Pages → Create).
2. Go to **business.facebook.com** → log in.
3. Settings → **Accounts → Instagram accounts → Add** → connect **@trigunaiinnovations**.
4. Confirm the IG account shows up linked to the Page.

*(If Business Suite won't let you schedule Reels, it's almost always because the IG↔Page
link isn't done — recheck step 3.)*

---

## STEP 1 — schedule one reel (repeat 12×)

1. **business.facebook.com** → left menu → **Planner** (calendar icon).
2. Click **Create post** (or click a date on the calendar) → choose **Reel**.
3. Under "Publish to", tick **Instagram** (untick Facebook if you only want IG — but posting
   to both is free reach, your call).
4. **Add video** → pick the reel file from the path in the table below.
5. Wait for it to process → pick a **cover frame** (choose a frame with a clear face/text).
6. Paste the **caption** (from the table) into the caption box.
7. Click **Schedule** (not Publish) → set the **date + 7:30 pm** → confirm.
8. Repeat for the next reel on the next day.

> Tip: do all 12 in one sitting. Business Suite lets you queue them all with different dates.
