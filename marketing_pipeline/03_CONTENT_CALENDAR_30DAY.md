## 0. The spine (read once, then just follow the calendar)

- **One conversion engine:** a **FREE LIVE INTRO CLASS every Saturday.** *Everything* each week
  points to that Saturday. Fill that room → 1:1 → bank transfer → Agentic Cohort 1.
- **Pillars & mix (pre-proof):** ~40% Wonder · 25% Build · 25% Origin · 10% Proof.
- **Spearhead = Agentic cohort** (3 of 4 warm leads chose it). VR/MR is the deepest *content* fuel
  (your shipped Quest app = the Origin/Proof story). Lead emotion with Agentic offer; use VR to prove "I built it."
- **Two languages:** every Short ships **EN + HI**. LinkedIn = EN (founder profile).
- **Cardinal rule:** never "buy" to a cold stranger. Wonder/Origin warm them → the Saturday class
  is where the offer lands.

## 3. Production batch plan (how a solo founder actually sustains this)

**Batch, don't make-daily.** Two sittings a week produce everything:

| When | ~Time | Batch task | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sun evening** | 1.5 hr | Cut the week's **3 Shorts** from existing episodes (EN+HI) via the production pipeline; write captions | 6 uploads queued |
| **Mon morning** | 1 hr | Write the week's **5 LinkedIn posts** in one sitting (this calendar gives you the hook + angle for each) | 5 posts scheduled |
| **Fri** | 30 min | Write + send the **week's email** (invite to Sat class); post the LinkedIn invite | room filled |
| **Sat** | 1 hr | **Run the live intro class** (the only "live" cost) | leads → 1:1s |

Everything else is scheduling. **No new footage required for Weeks 1–3** — the 14 bilingual episodes
are the Wonder/Build fuel. Record *one* fresh "Build an agent in 40 min" video only if Saturday classes
demand a replay asset.

**Scheduling tools:** LinkedIn native scheduler (or Buffer) · YouTube scheduled uploads ·
`trigunai-marketing` `publish.py` for the email + multi-channel fan-out.
