## 6. Production plan (how to actually make 15 heroes without dying)

**Batch in 2 sessions, not daily scramble:**
- **Batch A (before Day 1):** the 5 "hero reels" that need rendering — 2× "Ask Acharya" screen-records,
  1 robotics/Isaac clip, 1 multilingual, 1 recap. Use `production-video-trigunai` / screen capture of
  the real Acharya WhatsApp chat. Reuse the 11 already-made reels for the brand/episode/course days.
- **Batch B:** the 4 carousels (attention / VR / agentic / teacher) — make as 1080×1350 image sets;
  `content-marketing-emotion-connect` for copy, any slide tool for design.
- **Repurpose:** every IG reel → YouTube Short (same file) → the `trigunai-social-reels` skill posts
  IG+FB automatically; upload Shorts via `trigunai-yt-english`.
- **FlowArt music:** `shader-reactive-pattern-music` / `hypnotic-techno-trigunai` for the 2 drops.
- **LinkedIn:** company page API is **pending approval** → post LinkedIn **manually** for now (or via
  Buffer). Founder posts go from **Deepak's personal profile** (higher B2B reach).

**Posting time (IST):** IG/FB reels **7–9pm**; LinkedIn **8–10am** weekdays; Shorts anytime.
