## The arc (6 beats)

### Beat 0 — HOOK (3 min)
*Open an emotional loop, no slow intro.*
> "If you've sent 200 applications and heard nothing — the problem isn't you. You're applying
> into a job market that quietly changed *shape*. Tonight I'll show you the new shape, and the
> one move that puts you on the right side of it."

Drop the tension stat: **entry-level IT roles are down 20–25%** (AI automated them) — *but* AI
roles are heading past **1 million** with a **~53% skills gap.** The jobs didn't vanish. They moved.

### Beat 1 — THE LANDSCAPE (12 min)  *(use the Job Map as the visual)*
- **What died:** commodity entry-level work — the CRUD apps, ticket queues. AI does it now.
- **What's exploding:** **GCCs** (Global Capability Centers) — 64% increasing fresher hiring,
  freshers with GenAI/data skills at ₹10–18 LPA; GenAI/agent roles with *1 qualified engineer
  per 10 openings.*
- **The screen:** 60–70% of fresher JDs now require AI/ML proficiency — even non-AI roles.
- **The unlock:** GCCs hire freshers through **hackathons, internships, and projects — not
  resume volume.** → *Show, don't apply.*

### Beat 2 — THE REFRAME (8 min)  *(the identity shift — agency)*
> "You weren't trained too little. You were trained for the wrong thing. The market stopped
> paying for people who *use* software and started paying for people who *build with AI*.
> That skill is learnable — and far faster than the 4 years you already put in."

Name the exact builder skills (agents, RAG, LLM apps, MLOps) — from the Job Map Section 3.

### Beat 3 — LIVE BUILD (15 min)  *(the "oh — I could do this" moment, the heart of it)*
Build one tiny real thing **live**: a working AI agent that does a single task (e.g., answers
questions over a document via RAG). Narrate every step in plain language.
> Goal: they *watch a job-relevant thing get built in 15 minutes* and feel "that's not magic,
> that's learnable." This beat converts more than any slide.

### Beat 4 — THE FIRST MOVE (7 min)  *(hand them the map)*
- The ONE project to build this week (Job Map Section 4).
- How to show it: GitHub + a 60-second demo video.
- Where to target: GCCs, hackathons, the method (Job Map Section 5).
- **Give everyone the free "2026 AI Job Map" one-pager** (lead magnet → email capture).

### Beat 5 — THE BRIDGE (5 min)  *(the offer — Stage 4 only)*
> "Tonight was the *what*. If you want the *how* — hands-on, with a real NVIDIA GPU provided, a
> live cohort, and projects you actually ship until you're job-ready — that's what the cohort is.
> No pressure. The map alone will already move you."

Honest close: small live cohort (real scarcity), what's included, how to join. One CTA.

### Q&A (10 min)
Capture every objection (price/trust/timing) — it's gold for the funnel.
