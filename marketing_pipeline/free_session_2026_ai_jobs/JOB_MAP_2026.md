## 1. The shift, in one line

The commodity entry-level jobs (basic CRUD apps, ticket-queue work) are shrinking — **entry-level IT roles are already down 20–25%** as AI automates them. But that's only half the story:

**The work moved from people who *use* software to people who *build with AI*.** And there aren't enough of them — India has roughly **one qualified engineer for every ten open Generative-AI roles.** That gap is your opportunity.

---

## 2. Where the jobs are now

**🏢 Global Capability Centers (GCCs)** — the foreign companies running their tech in India — are the engine:
- **64% of GCCs plan to *increase* fresher hiring** (up to 20% more).
- India has ~120,000 AI professionals in GCCs today; demand is heading toward **1M+ AI roles**.
- Freshers with GenAI / data-engineering skills are landing **₹10–18 LPA** starting packages in premium (BFSI / tech) GCCs.
- **They hire through hackathons, internships, and projects — not resume volume.** This is the most important sentence on this page.

**🏬 Big IT (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL)** is still hiring (~82,000 grads in FY2026) — but selectively. **60–70% of fresher job descriptions now require AI/ML proficiency**, even for "normal" software roles. No AI skill = filtered out.

## 3. The 7 skills employers actually screen for

| # | Skill | Why it's hired |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Generative AI & LLMs** | #1 employer priority. Building apps *on top of* models. |
| 2 | **AI agents** (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, RAG) | The fastest-growing category — tool use, memory, multi-step autonomy. |
| 3 | **Prompt engineering** | High-impact; controls model quality + cost. |
| 4 | **MLOps & deployment** | Shift from *building* models to *running* them in production. |
| 5 | **Vector databases** (Chroma, Pinecone) | The backbone of RAG / retrieval systems. |
| 6 | **Cloud & infra integration** | Where the models actually run. |
| 7 | **Python** (the glue for all of the above) | Non-negotiable baseline. |

> Notice: these are **building** skills, not **using** skills. That's the whole game.

## 4. What to BUILD this week (pick ONE)

You get hired by *showing*, not applying. Build one of these, put it on GitHub, record a 60-second demo:

1. **An AI agent that does one real task** — reads your email and drafts replies, or answers questions over your own notes (RAG). Stack: Python + LangChain/LangGraph + a vector DB.
2. **A RAG app over a document set** — upload PDFs, ask questions, get cited answers. This *is* what GCCs build for BFSI/healthcare clients.
3. **A small ML project end-to-end** — train → deploy → a working demo URL. Proves you can ship, not just notebook.

One shipped project says more than 200 applications.

---

## 5. How to actually get in (the method)

- **Show, don't spray.** A GitHub repo + a 60-sec demo video beats a polished resume.
- **Target GCCs, not just big IT.** Search "GCC" + your city on Naukri/LinkedIn; follow GCC hiring hackathons.
- **Lead with the build.** In every message/application, link the project first, marks last.
- **Speak their language** — use the exact skill words from Section 3 in your profile and project README.
