## Full company description (for deck/website)

Trigunaï Innovations is an early-stage deep-tech company building the connective tissue of
physical AI for visual capture. We combine motion-capture data, reinforcement learning, and
VR validation to train autonomous camera policies — software cinematographers that learn from
data and deploy to real hardware. Our current focus is dance, performance, and live event
cinematography. We work with mocap libraries, train custom policies, and deliver them in
formats that drop into both creative pipelines and drone hardware.
