### 4. Video Rendering — Complete (after debugging)
- Script: `cinematography/render_trained_cinematographer.py`
- Renderer: OVRTX on EC2 port 8001 (batch rendering, 30 frames/batch)
- Coordinate transform: Isaac Z-up → USD Y-up: `(x,y,z) → (x,z,-y)`

#### Bugs fixed during rendering:
| Bug | Symptom | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missing USDA headers | Blank frames | No `defaultPrim`, `upAxis`, `metersPerUnit` | Added all three (matching proven `bake_dancer_usda.py` format) |
| Wrong light syntax | Dark scene | `float intensity` instead of `float inputs:intensity` | Matched proven format |
| Camera rotation sign | Camera pointing at sky | `rx = atan2(-dy, horiz)` gives positive when camera above target, but positive rx = look UP in USD | Fixed to `rx = atan2(dy, horiz)` (negative = look down) |
| OVRTX daemon corruption | All renders return blank | Rapid scene switching pollutes daemon state | `docker restart ovrtx-rendering-api` (6 min cold start) |
| OVRTX timeout on large batches | 150-frame single request times out at 300s | Too many frames per POST | Split into 30-frame batches |

### 6. Key architecture decisions
- **Drone = Camera**: the trained drone trajectory IS the camera path. No separate camera RL needed.
- **Static camera for debug, drone-POV for delivery**: overhead static cam verifies the trajectory geometry; drone-POV is the actual product output.
- **Stick-figure dancer**: orange body sphere + skin-tone head sphere. Full mocap retargeting (Daphne) is a later phase.
- **USDA format must exactly match `bake_dancer_usda.py`**: that script's header/lights/camera format is the only proven-to-render template for OVRTX.

## Physics-based lower body prediction (planned — after A4)

User requested physics-based lower body prediction from Quest upper body data using Isaac Sim. Architecture designed:

- **Pinned upper body AMP env**: new `Isaac-Humanoid-AMP-LowerBody-Direct-v0` task
- **DOF split**: 14 upper body DOFs pinned to Quest data (high-stiffness PD), 14 lower body DOFs controlled by RL policy
- **AMP discriminator**: trained on full-body reference motions (`humanoid_dance.npz` built-in) to enforce natural leg motion
- **Reward**: 30% AMP naturalness + 20% balance + 15% foot contact + 10% anti-slide + 10% energy + 5% upright + 10% root height
- **Training recipe**: 256 envs on A10G, 500 iters for validation, 3000 iters for full training
- **Key files**: `humanoid_amp_lower_body_env.py`, `humanoid_amp_lower_body_env_cfg.py`, `quest_to_upper_body_dof.py`

Full plan in planning agent output. Implementation starts after A4 gate approval.
