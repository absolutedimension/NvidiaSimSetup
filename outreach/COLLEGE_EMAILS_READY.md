## Sending discipline
- **One email at a time**, each with the college name + one specific personalised line. No bulk BCC blast (→ spam, and it reads as mass mail to a placement officer).
- Send during **working hours**, Tue–Thu best for B2B opens.
- Log every reply in COLLEGE_OUTREACH_PATNA_ARA.md so we learn which framing/college converts.
- **Goal of wave 1 = book 1–2 free guest sessions.** A booked session is the real signal, not "emails sent."
