## Rules for sending

1. **Personalize the first line.** Reference their specific work. Generic = delete.
2. **Attach the MP4 directly** if emailing. If DM/LinkedIn, host on YouTube (unlisted) or Google Drive and link.
3. **Do NOT attach the one-pager unsolicited.** It's for the second message, after they reply.
4. **Track every send** in a simple spreadsheet: name, channel, date sent, reply Y/N, conversation Y/N, what they said.
5. **Goal is learning, not selling.** The first 5 conversations teach you whether the wedge is right, what the real pain is, and what language prospects use. That's worth more than a sale right now.
