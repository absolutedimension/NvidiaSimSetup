## Recommended send plan (don't blast 60 at once)
- **Wave 1 (today): the 11 ⭐ TPO inboxes** — highest leverage, real placement officers, lowest spam risk.
- **Wave 2 (next day): the ~28 other OFFICIAL inboxes.**
- **Wave 3: Tier 2 directory emails** — phone-verify a quick batch first.
- **Deliverability:** send ~10–15/day, not 60 in one burst — a normal Gmail cold-sending 60 at once gets throttled/spam-flagged. Spread over 3–4 days, one-by-one or tiny BCC groups, personalised.
- **The win is a booked free session, not "emails sent."** Log replies; the colleges that reply are wave-1 of the real funnel.
