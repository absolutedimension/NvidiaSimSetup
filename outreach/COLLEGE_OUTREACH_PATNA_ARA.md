   (GEC Bhojpur in Arrah, LNJPIT Chhapra). If the goal is *reaching students*, the Ara play is
   government colleges + polytechnics — not "private." Patna carries the private-college density.
2. **Every email below needs verification before you send.** Officially-verified addresses are
   tagged ✅OFFICIAL; the rest are ✅DIR (from a third-party directory — confirm by a 30-sec phone
   call asking for "the Training & Placement Officer's email" before mailing). No address was invented.
   For small Bihar colleges, directory gmail addresses go stale — **phone-verify the gmail ones.**
