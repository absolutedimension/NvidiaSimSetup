# TrigunAI — Course Pricing & Duration Model (2026-06-20)

> The *why* behind every number. Market-researched, tailored to a solo founder. Companion to the
> landing handoff (`landing-page-handoff/MASTER_LANDING_HANDOFF.md`).

## The principle
**Don't price by duration. Price by outcome + delivery intensity.** Lengthening a course to justify a
higher price backfires — it raises drop-off *and* the founder's delivery load. The lever for a higher
price is more **live/high-touch + a tangible shipped outcome**, not more weeks.

## Market reality (India, 2026 — researched)
- **Recorded/self-paced:** ₹500–₹5,000. 10–15% completion. Race to the bottom.
- **Solo-creator live cohort / coaching: ₹15,000–₹40,000 — our lane.**
- **Branded bootcamps:** ₹50k–₹4L (GrowthSchool ~₹50–70k; Scaler ₹2–3L job-track). Not our lane yet.
- **Decisive fact:** cohorts get **85–90% completion vs 10–15%** self-paced, and people pay ~**3× more for
  the same content live** — accountability + structure + a shipped outcome is the value, not the hours.
- EMI is standard and expected in India.

## The 3-rung ladder (per course)
1. **Recorded self-paced** — ₹3,999–₹5,999. The passive floor + funnel. No live, no GPU, no support.
2. **Live cohort (THE ENGINE)** — ₹17,999–₹49,999 by depth. EMI. Provided GPU, live debugging, certificate.
3. **(Later) 1:1 / B2B team training** — ₹75k+. Not now.

## The model

| Course | Audience | Shape · Duration | Cohort | EMI | Recorded | Founding (first cohort) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build & Ship a VR/MR App | career/indie | Live · 8 wk | ₹34,999 | 3×₹11,999 | ₹5,999 | ₹24,999 |
| **Build Agentic AI Systems** | dev/operator | Live · 3 mo | **₹35,000** (locked C1) | 3×₹12,000 | ₹4,999 | — (C1 *is* founding) |
| Machine Learning & Its Math | dev/curious | Live · 10 wk | ₹29,999 | 3×₹9,999 | ₹4,999 | ₹21,999 |
| Physical AI — Train a Robot | intermediate | Cohort + hardware · 8 wk | ₹49,999 | 4×₹12,999 | — | ₹39,999 |
| **Build Your AI Video Factory** | **creators** | **Intensive · 15–21 days** | ₹17,999 | 2×₹9,499 | ₹3,999 | **₹12,999** |
| **Build Your AI Music Factory** | **creators** | **Intensive · 15–21 days** | ₹17,999 | 2×₹9,499 | ₹3,999 | **₹12,999** |

**Why creator courses (Video/Music) are short + a notch cheaper:** different buyer. Creators want
*speed-to-income*, are busier and more price-sensitive than career-switchers. 15–21 days is the right
*intensity*; 3 months would kill conversion. Dev/career courses earn their length (and ₹30–50k) because
the skill is deeper and the outcome (job/portfolio) supports the commitment.

## Two de-risking tactics
- **Founding-cohort price:** run each new course's *first* cohort ~30% off in exchange for a testimonial +
  case study. Cheaper first sale, builds proof, then raise to full.
- **₹2,000–₹5,000 weekend workshops** as paid lead-magnets that upsell into the cohorts. *This* is the
  "short + cheap" play — a feeder, not the flagship.

## The honest gate
**0 rupees have cleared.** The 3 Agentic students registered at ₹35k but haven't paid. The real pricing
research is **whether those 3 actually transfer ₹35k.** Set the model from this table; let the first
cohort's behavior — including any hesitation at the bank-transfer step — tune it. Hold prices for Cohort 1.
