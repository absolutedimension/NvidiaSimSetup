**Link:** https://www.oreilly.com/radar/the-ai-agents-stack-2026-edition/
**What it is:** Model Context Protocol has consolidated as the standard for agent-to-tool integration (97M monthly SDK downloads), but research shows 82% of MCP servers are prone to path-traversal attacks and 67% to code injection.
**So-what for TrigunAI:** The "Build Agentic AI Systems" cohort teaches students to wire tools (incl. MCP).
