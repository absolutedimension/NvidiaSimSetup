## 1. What you're building

A single, premium, mobile-first landing page for **trigunai.com**. It does three things, in order:
1. **Tells the brand story** — "AI is the Universal Mind" — with wonder, not hype.
2. **Sends people to the free YouTube series** (the funnel) and captures their **email** (Substack).
3. **Showcases the courses** so a visitor can **register interest** (free waitlist — see §6).

No backend, no login, no checkout. The page is a doorway to: the series, an email list, and course waitlists.

---

## 2. The brand story (the spine of every word on the page)

**The thesis:** *AI is the Universal Mind.* Every AI breakthrough is humanity accidentally rebuilding
one faculty of its own mind — attention became transformers, memory became embeddings, intuition
became neural nets. So **AI is a mirror, not a copy**: a tool that makes the invisible machinery of
*your own mind* visible. Look closely at one, and you understand the other.

**Hold the thesis at three layers (don't collapse them):**
- **Poetic** (the title, the soul): "AI is the Universal Mind." This is the hook that travels.
- **Operational** (what we actually demonstrate): AI is the clearest *mirror* we've built for the
  mind — shown faculty by faculty, episode by episode. This is the defensible claim.
- **Stance:** *agnostic but pointed* — we hold the big question open with wonder; we don't preach,
  and we don't claim to have "proven" anything. A lens that keeps illuminating, not a dogma.

**The business spine (informs which CTA is soft vs hard):** *Free to understand, paid to transform.*
The understanding is free (the series). The money — later — is for those who want to *build/embody* it.

## 6. The funnel right now — EVERYTHING IS FREE (important)

Per Deepak (2026-06-14): **all episodes are free and public, nothing is gated.** All **5 episodes are
live on both channels (English + Hindi).** So today the page's hard ask is **attention + an email**, not money.

- **Primary CTA (soft, everywhere):** *Subscribe / Watch* — "all episodes, free."
- **Course CTA (the hard ask, per course):** **free waitlist**, not a price. Buttons read
  **"Get early access — free for now"** or **"Join the waitlist."** They capture name + email +
  which course = the demand signal, at zero cost to the visitor.
- **No price numbers on the page.** Courses go paid later; founding subscribers get first access —
  that's the reward for joining free now.

This is intentional: free maximizes reach (the curious audience's whole job) and builds the email
list, which is the asset we keep. **Two CTA intents** (Subscribe = soft; Register = hard) — keep them
visually distinct; don't add a third competing action.

## 9. Guardrails — do NOT

- **No inflated claims / no invented proof.** True today: Deepak shipped a VR app to Meta's platform
  (alpha); trains real policies on NVIDIA GPUs; **5 episodes live (EN + HI)**. NOT true and never shown:
  student counts, testimonials, "trusted by", revenue, "thousands of learners". If there's no number, don't show one.
- **Clarity, not healing.** Offer "see how your mind works," never therapy/cure claims. Stay rigorous, not woo.
- **No price numbers** while courses are free-waitlist. Use "waitlist", not a price.
- **No Quest-bundle pricing or hardware promises.** VR is a course topic, not a hardware store.
- **No hustle/"make money with faceless AI videos" energy** anywhere — off-brand, cheapens everything.
- **Don't resurrect the dead direction:** the old "Creator (Video SaaS) vs Student" page with ₹999/₹4,999
  tiers is dead. Ignore it. This page is **series → subscribe → courses (free waitlist)**.
