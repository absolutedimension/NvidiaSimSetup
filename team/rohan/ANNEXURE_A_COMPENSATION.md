## A.1 Fixed Monthly Retainer — ₹10,000
- Paid every month for meeting the **minimum activity bar**:
  - **Minimum 100 verified calls per week** to assigned leads (≈ 20 working days/month), **AND**
  - **Same-day logging** of every call and visit in the Company tracker.
- If the activity bar is not met for reasons within your control, the Company may pro-rate the retainer for that month (discussed and agreed in writing before deduction).
- The retainer is **not** additional to a conversion — it is your base for showing up and doing the work.

## A.2 Conversion Incentive — ₹1,100 per successful onboarding
This is the core earning, and it is **uncapped**.

**"Successful onboarding" means ALL of these:**
1. A teacher/institute **you** sourced or handled agrees to the Acharya plan, **AND**
2. Their Acharya instance is **set up and live**, **AND**
3. Their **first monthly payment (₹4,999) is received and cleared** in the official TrigunAI account.

- The ₹1,100 is credited in the month the **first payment clears**.
- If a teacher signs up for the free 14-day pilot but never pays, **no incentive is due** (the pilot alone is not a conversion).
- If a converted teacher cancels/refunds within the first payment cycle, that conversion incentive may be reversed/withheld.

## A.3 Field-Visit Pay — ₹200 per visit (fixed) + ₹500 bonus on conversion
Covers your travel & effort for in-person field visits, with an extra reward when a visit converts.

**(a) Fixed visit pay — ₹200 per verified visit (paid regardless of outcome).**
A visit is "verified" and paid only if ALL are true:
1. The institute is on the Company's **assigned/approved lead list** (not self-picked, unless pre-approved), **AND**
2. You submit **proof**: a photo at the institute (board/nameplate visible) **plus** the visit form filled — institute name, owner/decision-maker name, phone, what was discussed, outcome, **AND**
3. The visit is **logged the same day**.

**Monthly cap on fixed visit pay:** up to **25 verified visits/month (₹5,000 max)**. Genuine visits beyond 25 may be approved case-by-case in writing.

**(b) Converted-visit bonus — extra ₹500** when a verified in-person visit becomes a **successful onboarding** under A.2 (first ₹4,999 cleared). Uncapped (each requires a real paying customer).

**So, per outcome:**
| Outcome | Pay |
|---|---|
| Verified visit, did **not** convert | **₹200** |
| Verified visit that **converts** | ₹200 + ₹500 + ₹1,100 = **₹1,800** |
| Conversion closed **on phone only** (no visit) | **₹1,100** |

- Payouts are against **verified records only** (call log + visit proof + confirmed cleared payment).
- The Company may revise this structure with **15 days' written notice**; existing earned incentives are protected.
