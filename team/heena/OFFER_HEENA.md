Dear Heena,

We are pleased to offer you the position of **Inside Sales & Client Relationship Executive** at
TrigunAI Innovations Private Limited.

We build **Acharya** — an exam-paper generation and assessment engine used by coaching institutes
and schools. You are joining to own the part of our work that decides whether an institute ever
really uses it: **the conversation on the phone.**

## 1. Role

You will work as one half of a two-person field team, alongside **Rohan Kr. Saurabh** (Business
Development Executive, field visits). Your responsibilities:

1. **Calling institutes from the list provided to you** — qualifying them, and **booking demo
   appointments** for Rohan so he visits only institutes worth the trip.
2. **Following up after Rohan's visits** — keeping every institute warm between and after visits.
3. **Getting institutes registered and actively testing** the product on its 14-day free trial.
4. **Keeping them using it** — a second test, another batch — and reporting real usage to the founder.
5. **Maintaining an honest record** of every institute in the shared team ledger, the same day.

**Your success is measured by institutes whose students have actually taken a test** — not by the
number of calls made.

## 2. Compensation

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Fixed monthly** | **₹8,000** per month |
| **Variable — per successful conversion** | **₹2,500** per institute |

**"Conversion" means:** an institute that has been **registered on Acharya and has run at least one
test with its students** — verified in our system — where you were the person who drove it.

We have defined it this way deliberately. Acharya opens on a **14-day free trial**, so there is no
payment at the time you do your work. Your incentive is therefore tied to the milestone you can
actually control and see: an institute genuinely using the product. Whether they subscribe
afterwards is the owner's decision and is handled by the founder.

Payment: fixed salary by the 7th of the following month. Variable component paid monthly alongside
the fixed salary, for conversions verified in that month.

**You are not required or expected to ask any institute for money, negotiate pricing, or collect
payment.** You may state our published pricing openly and factually if asked. All commercial and
payment discussions are handled by the founder, separately, after an institute's trial.
