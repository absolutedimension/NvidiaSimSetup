— THE SOUND ADVANTAGE (what the research actually says) —
• ISOCHRONIC TONES are a single tone pulsed at an even rhythm; the brain tends to synchronize to that pulse (the "frequency-following response" / brainwave entrainment), and they're considered well-suited to entraining the higher BETA band.
• BETA waves (~13–30 Hz) are associated with active concentration, alertness, and engaged problem-solving — the state you want for studying and deep work.
• A review of entrainment studies concluded binaural beats & isochronic tones "may modulate mood and improve attention and memory," with beta entrainment specifically linked to better attention. Steady, lyric-free music also reduces verbal interference and sustains focus.
• Honest note: this research is promising but limited and inconsistent — results vary by person. A focus aid, not a magic switch. Not medical advice.
