What makes anything — a person or a machine — want something? Episode 6 is about WILL:
how AI agents get a goal and pursue it across time, and what that reveals about your own drive.

In this episode:
• Wanting is a gap — the distance between where you are and where you aim
• A will, written as a number: the objective / reward function
• Reinforcement learning: it learns to pursue, not just react
• An agent = a loop with a target; planning = imagining branching futures
• Where the analogy breaks: you can question your own wanting; the machine can't
