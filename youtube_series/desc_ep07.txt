How does anything — a person or a machine — actually get better? Episode 7 is about LEARNING
FROM ERROR: gradient descent and backpropagation, and why your mistakes teach more than your wins.

In this episode:
• It starts with the error — measuring how wrong you are
• Which way is "less wrong"? The slope (gradient) points downhill
• Gradient descent: walking a landscape blindfolded, one step at a time
• Backpropagation: sending the error backward to fix every part
• The bigger the error, the bigger the correction — adjust in proportion
• Where the analogy breaks, and what stays true for you too
