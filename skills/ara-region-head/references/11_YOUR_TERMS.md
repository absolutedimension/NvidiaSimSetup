## 1. Your compensation

| Component | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed** | **₹10,000 / month** | — |
| **Per visit** | **₹200** | Per **verified, logged** visit. Capped at **25 visits/month** (max ₹5,000). |
| **Per converted visit** | **₹500** | The institute agrees to a pilot and a batch goes live |
| **Per first payment** | **₹1,100** | The institute's first payment actually reaches TrigunAI |

**So a normal working month is ₹10,000–15,000 before conversions**, and the upside sits entirely on
the two things that matter: **batches that go live**, and **money that arrives.**

---

## 2. The two rules behind those numbers

**A · Paid against verified logs only.**
No log = no visit = no payment. Not a technicality — it is the only way Deepak can see the region
without standing in it. Log the same day, every day, including the nos.

A "verified" visit means the row exists in `ara_institutes.csv` with: institute, area, date, who you
met, what they said about test papers, and the next step. Attach a proof point when you can — a
photo of the office, the owner's card.

**B · The bonus sits on installations, not interest.**
₹500 is for a batch that went live. Not for an owner who liked it, not for a promised start date.
This is deliberate: you get paid for what the company actually needs, and it protects you from
spending a month on people who were only being polite.

## 3. No cash — ever

You never collect money from an institute. Payments go **only** to the official TrigunAI link, which
Deepak sends directly to them. This protects you as much as it protects the company.

---

## 4. What you may pay a field hire

**Same structure, same numbers** — ₹10,000 fixed + ₹200/verified visit (cap 25/mo) + ₹500/converted
visit + ₹1,100/first payment.

**Confirm each hire's package with Deepak before you offer it.** You choose who to hire; he
approves the money. Never agree pay with someone before you have his yes in writing — reversing it
afterwards costs you their trust permanently.

And hold the same two rules with them that Deepak holds with you: **verified logs only**, and **the
bonus on batches live, not on interest.** The first exception you make to the logging rule destroys
the whole system, so make none.
