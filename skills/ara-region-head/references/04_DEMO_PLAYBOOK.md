# 04 · The demo — 3 minutes, in the room

> **The one principle: people believe what they DO, not what they watch.**
> The entire demo is *"hand them the phone."* You talk less. The owner's own fingers, on his own
> exam, on a chapter **he** chose, is the sale. A polished video is the *weakest* demo — it is your
> parachute for no-signal, nothing more.

---

## Before you walk in — the 30-second pre-check

Never let a demo die on friction. Every morning, before you leave:

- ✅ Open **`acharya.trigunai.com/exam-prep`** on your phone and **generate one paper yourself.**
  Today. Not yesterday. If it works, you walk in relaxed; if it's broken, you find out alone
  instead of in front of an owner.
- ✅ Phone charged. Mobile data on. Screen brightness up (you'll be handing it over in a bright room).
- ✅ You know which exam this owner teaches, and you have one chapter in your head ready to suggest
  if he freezes.
- ✅ You already did discovery — **you demo the pain he just named**, not a feature tour.
- ✅ The Hindi one-page leave-behind is in your bag.

---

## THE 3-MINUTE FLOW — run it the same way every time

### Step 1 · Recall their pain (10 seconds)

Tie the demo to what **he** just told you. Never start with "let me show you our product."

> *"Sir, aapne abhi bataya — har hafte paper banane mein teen-chaar ghante jaate hain. Ek minute,
> main dikhata hoon."*

### Step 2 · Hand him the phone — HE chooses (60 seconds)

This is the whole demo. **Do not tap it yourself.**

> *"Aap khud chuniye sir — kaunsa exam? TRE? SSC? Railway?"*
> *"Ab subject… aur chapter bhi aap hi chuniye. Polity? History?"*

Let him pick. **The fact that he chose it is the entire proof** — a demo you drove is a video; a
demo he drove is his product. Then stay quiet and let the paper appear.

### Step 3 · The line that lands (20 seconds)

As the paper comes up on screen — this is the most important sentence in the whole job:

> *"Sir, ye AI ka banaya hua nahi hai. Ye ASLI TRE papers ke asli questions hain — asli answer key
> ke saath. Do hazaar se zyada. Aur aap jitne chahein paper nikaal sakte hain, kisi bhi chapter se."*

**Real past questions + unlimited papers.** That is the pitch. Do not add features to it.

### Step 4 · Show him HIS name on it (20 seconds)

The thing that makes it *his*:

> *"Aur ye paper aapke institute ke naam se chhapega — aapka logo, aapka naam. Bilkul free, aap khud
> set kar sakte hain."*

For a small-town owner competing with Patna, **his name printed on a professional test paper is
emotional, not functional.** Don't rush this part.

### Step 5 · Don't end it — START it (30 seconds)

Never ask *"kaisa laga?"* That question invites *"sochkar batata hoon."* Assume the pilot:

> *"Chaliye, aaj hi aapke ek batch pe laga dete hain — aapke naam se. Free hai, paisa month ke baad.
> Aap khud dekh lijiye ki students use karte hain ya nahi."*

**Capture before you leave — all four, written down:**
1. Batch size
2. Which exam
3. Start date
4. **The name of the person who will run it daily** ← the one everyone forgets, and the one that
   decides whether this becomes a real pilot or a nice memory

## Demo variations — match the owner in front of you

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Busy owner ("do minute hai")** | Steps 2 + 3 only. He picks a chapter, paper appears, you say the "asli questions" line. **90 seconds.** |
| **Skeptic ("AI se padhai nahi hoti")** | **Agree with him.** *"Bilkul sahi kaha sir — AI padha nahi sakta. Isliye hum padhane ki baat kar hi nahi rahe. Ye sirf paper banata hai — wo bhi asli purane papers se."* Reframing to a mechanical task defuses the whole objection. |
| **"Mere paas already Classplus hai"** | *"Bahut achha. Usme test banane ka option hai na? Usme questions kitne hain?"* — the answer is *none*, the owner supplies them himself. We fill his empty module; we don't replace his app. |
| **No signal / it glitches** | Do not fumble. Switch to the printed sample paper in your bag, then: *"main aapko link bhej deta hoon, aap khud try kijiye."* ⚠️ **Carry a printed sample paper of the exam he teaches** — in Ara this is more reliable than a video. |
| **Owner won't touch the phone** | You tap, but narrate it as HIS institute: *"maan lijiye aapke TRE batch ka test hai is Sunday…"* Still his exam, still his chapter. |
| **He's a solo teacher, not an institute** | Same demo, different close: *"Aapko institute banane ki zaroorat nahi — aapke naam se test series chal jayegi. ₹250 mahina."* |

---

## What NOT to do — these kill demos

- ❌ **Don't tap it yourself if he'll touch the phone.** His fingers = belief. Yours = a pitch.
- ❌ **Don't demo the wrong exam.** Always HIS exam. A JEE paper shown to a TRE institute means
  *"ye mere kaam ka nahi."*
- ❌ **Don't feature-tour.** Demo the ONE pain he named. Everything else is noise that dilutes it.
- ❌ **Don't demo anything that isn't live.** If he asks for something we don't have — attendance,
  fee collection, parent SMS — say *"abhi ye nahi hai"* and **log it.** Never mock up a fake screen.
- ❌ **Don't over-talk.** After you hand him the phone, **shut up.** Silence plus his own experience
  closes better than anything you can say.
- ❌ **Don't beg for a verdict.** Go straight to "chaliye ek batch pe laga dete hain."
- ❌ **Don't promise marks or results.** Ever. *"Zyada practice, aur progress dikhta hai."*
