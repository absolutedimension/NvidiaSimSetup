# 09 · Your first 15 days

> One goal for the whole fortnight: **one Ara institute with a live batch whose students actually
> took a test.** Everything below serves that. Not five signed pilots, not a big list — **one real
> installation**, because the first one teaches you the motion and gives you a name to drop with
> every institute after it.

---

## Days 1–2 · Learn the product properly (at home)

- [ ] Read the kit in the order given in `SKILL.md` §1 (≈90 min)
- [ ] **Generate five test papers yourself** on `acharya.trigunai.com/exam-prep` — TRE, SSC, Railway,
      Class 10, Class 12. Different chapters each time.
- [ ] **Print one.** Hold it. That printed sheet is what you are actually selling in Ara, and you
      should know what it looks and feels like before an owner does.
- [ ] Try to break it — pick an obscure chapter, an odd combination. **Whatever breaks, tell Deepak.**
      Finding a bug in week 1 is a contribution, not a complaint.
- [ ] Run the demo on a friend or family member, out loud, twice. It should take 3 minutes.

**Do not visit an institute in these two days.** A weak first demo in a small town is expensive —
word travels, and you only get one first impression per owner.

## Days 3–4 · Build the list

- [ ] Google Maps sweep — every search string in `03_ARA_SOURCING_GUIDE.md` §A, both spellings
      (Ara **and** Arrah)
- [ ] **Target: 40+ names in `ara_institutes.csv`** with name, area, phone, subject
- [ ] Walk two clusters in the evening; photograph signboards; transcribe the same night
- [ ] Call/message three people in your own network for warm introductions
- [ ] Correct the cluster table in `02_ARA_MARKET_BRIEF.md` §4 with what you actually saw

**The list is the region.** This is unglamorous and it is the highest-leverage work of the fortnight.

## Days 5–8 · First visits (target: 12 real conversations)

- [ ] **Start with the two app-owning institutes** — PANDEY CLASSES ARA and Physics World Ara. They
      already pay for software. (`03_ARA_SOURCING_GUIDE.md` §D)
- [ ] ONE cluster per day — 3–4 institutes you can walk between
- [ ] Discovery first, every time. Ten minutes of them talking before you show anything.
- [ ] Log every single one the same day, including the nos, with their exact reason
- [ ] Aim for **3 real conversations a day**. Depth over count.

**By day 8 you should be able to answer, from your own field notes:** who writes test papers in Ara,
how long it takes them, and what they currently use. If you can't, you have been pitching instead of
listening — go back to `07_FIELD_RESEARCH_GUIDE.md`.

## Days 9–12 · Convert one pilot into a live batch

- [ ] Go back to the two or three warmest from days 5–8. **The second visit closes more than the
      first** — small-town trust is built on returning.
- [ ] Get one batch live: batch size · exam · start date · **the name of the person who runs it daily**
- [ ] Sit with that person once and watch them generate a paper themselves. Do not do it for them.
      An institute where only *you* can operate it will churn the day you stop visiting.
- [ ] Make sure a test actually happens that week. Chase it. This is the whole fortnight.

## Days 13–15 · Prove it and report

- [ ] Confirm students took a test — how many, which exam, what the dashboard showed
- [ ] Get a photo of the printed paper with the institute's name on it (**ask permission**) — this is
      your single best selling asset for every subsequent visit in Ara
- [ ] Ask the owner the referral question: *"aapke jaan-pehchaan mein aur kaun bada batch chalata hai?"*
- [ ] Send Deepak the fortnight report (§below)
- [ ] **Only now**, open `08_TEAM_BUILD_PLAYBOOK.md`

---

## Your fortnight report to Deepak — five lines, nothing more

```
1. Institutes in the list:        __
2. Real conversations:            __
3. Demos given:                   __
4. Batches LIVE (students took a test this week):  __
5. The three most common things owners said about test papers (their words):
6. HINDI: of the owners you asked — how many said English-only would BLOCK them? __ / __
```

Line 6 is a live product decision waiting on you. The papers are English-only today. Building
Hindi is about a week of work, and Deepak will only spend it if Ara owners say English-only
stops them using it. Ask both halves of the question (`SKILL.md` §3) and report the count, not
your impression.

Line 4 is the only one that counts. Lines 1–3 explain line 4; they do not substitute for it.

---

## What "good" looks like at day 15

**Good:** 40+ institutes listed, ~12 conversations, **1 batch live with a real test taken**, and you
can quote three owners verbatim on their test-paper pain.

**Not good, even though it feels good:** 60 institutes listed, 25 visits, eight owners who "loved
it", zero batches live. That is the exact shape of a wasted fortnight, and it is the most common way
this job goes wrong — because every day of it feels productive.

**Also good, and worth saying:** one batch live and *nine clear nos with real reasons.* The nos are
data the company does not currently have, and they are why we hired someone who lives there.
