## 1. Why Ara is a different market from Patna — and why that is good news

Rohan works Patna. Patna is a **JEE/NEET city**: premium institutes, digital-savvy owners, national
chains on every corner, and a lot of competition for the owner's attention.

**Ara is a govt-exam town.** ⚠️ Read: the money and the emotion in Bhojpur sit in

- **BPSC TRE** (teacher recruitment) — huge, and it is *the* aspiration for a graduate in Bhojpur
- **SSC** (CGL / CHSL / MTS)
- **Railway** (NTPC, Group D, ALP) — Ara sits on the main line; railway jobs are a family goal
- **Bihar Police / Daroga / BSSC**
- **Bihar Board** (Class 10 / 12) tuition, in volume
- JEE/NEET exists, but the serious aspirants often **leave for Patna or Kota**

**This is the best possible news for us, and here is why:** the exam we have the deepest, most
credible bank for is **BPSC TRE — 2,026 real past-paper questions with real answer keys.** We built
it in the last two weeks for a Patna institute. In Ara, that bank is not a nice-to-have; it is the
main thing the town is studying for.

> **Your headline advantage over every competitor in Ara:**
> *"TRE ke do hazaar se zyada ASLI past-paper questions — asli answer key ke saath. Chapter-wise.
> Aap jitne paper chahein nikaal sakte hain."*

Nobody else in Ara is handing an owner that.

## 2. The second wedge — "aapke students Patna bhaag rahe hain"

⚠️ The structural anxiety of a small-city institute owner is that his best students leave for Patna,
and he loses both the fee and the reputation. He usually cannot compete on faculty or building.

**He can compete on rigour** — weekly tests, real past-paper questions, a printed paper with his own
name on it, and a report showing each student where they are weak. That is exactly what we give him,
for ₹150–250 a month.

> *"Sir, Patna ke institute ka faayda kya hai? Test series. Aapke paas wahi test series aa jaaye —
> aapke naam se, asli TRE questions ke saath — to student yahin rukega."*

Test that line early. If it lands with two owners, it becomes your standard opener.

## 3. The Play Store finding — and what it tells you

In Patna, our best lead source is a Play Store sweep: an institute with its own branded app has
already paid ₹4–11 lakh for a white-label platform, so the owner is pre-qualified on budget and on
willingness to buy software — and those platforms hand him a test module with **no questions in it**.

**We ran the same sweep on Ara on 2026-08-18. It found almost nothing.** Two real institutes with
apps:

| Institute | Platform | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **PANDEY CLASSES ARA** | Classplus (reseller) | 45 reviews, maths-heavy, reviews praise step-by-step solutions |
| **Physics World Ara** | self-built | 52 reviews, Physics 11/12 |

Everything else the sweep returned was a false match on the letters "ara" (Aravali, Aarav, Aranyak,
Aparajita, ARA Finserv — a finance company). Raw output: `ara_playstore_leads.csv`.

**Two conclusions, both actionable:**

1. **Ara is an on-foot market.** No scraper will build your list. Google Maps by locality, signboards,
   and your own network will. See `03_ARA_SOURCING_GUIDE.md`.
2. **The pitch changes.** In Patna we say *"your test module is empty."* In Ara, most owners have
   **no software at all** — so the conversation is not about replacing a tool, it is about the
   3 hours a week they currently spend cutting and pasting questions from a guidebook. That is a
   simpler, more emotional sell. Lean into the time, not the technology.

And the two app-owning institutes above are your **highest-priority first visits** — they have
already proved they will pay for software.

## 5. Your ideal customer (ICP) — who to spend time on

**Target:** small to mid, **owner-run** institutes of roughly **20–300 students**, teaching a
govt exam or board, where **the owner is in the building and decides the money on the spot.**

**Why owner-run matters:** he can say yes today. A branch manager cannot.

**Skip:** the big national chains (Aakash, Allen, PW, FIITJEE, Goal, Khan Sir-scale operations). No
local buying authority, and a month of your life disappears into their process.

**Also worth your time in Ara, unlike Patna:**
- **Solo star teachers** running their own batch of 40–100 from a rented room. In a govt-exam town
  these are everywhere, the whole decision is one person, and ₹250/month is nothing to them.
- **Library / self-study centres** — Ara has these; they have students and no content. A test series
  is a straight upgrade to what they sell.
