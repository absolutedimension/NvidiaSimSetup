## E · Before you pitch — qualify, then diagnose

**Pitch only if 3 of these 4 are true:**

- [ ] ≥10 current paying students
- [ ] Teaches an exam-prep subject (govt exam / board / NEET / JEE)
- [ ] Named a real pain when you asked about test papers
- [ ] The person in front of you owns the money decision

Not qualified → thank them, ask *"aapke jaan-pehchaan mein koi bada batch chalata hai?"*, log it, leave.

**The three questions that matter:**

1. *"Aapke test papers abhi kaun banata hai, aur ek paper mein kitna time lagta hai?"*
2. *"Kitni baar test hota hai — aur kabhi paper ready na hone se test skip hua hai?"*
3. *"Test ke baad kaise pata chalta hai ki poora batch kis chapter mein kamzor hai?"*

Whichever makes him talk fast is the pain you sell to.

**And the one Deepak needs answered from Ara** — the papers are English-only today:

> *"Abhi paper English mein aata hai. Aapke students ko Hindi mein chahiye, ya English chal jayega?"*
> *"Agar English hi rahe, to aap phir bhi use karenge — ya rukega?"*

The second half decides it. *"Hindi hota to achha hota"* is a wish; **"Hindi ke bina nahi chalega"
is a requirement** — and only that justifies building it. Log the exact words.
