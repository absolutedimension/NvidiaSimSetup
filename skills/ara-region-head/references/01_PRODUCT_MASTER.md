# 01 · Product Master — what Acharya actually is

> **Read this twice before your first visit.** You cannot sell a product you can only describe from
> a brochure. By the end of this page you should be able to answer, without looking anything up:
> *what does it do, what does it not do, and why would an Ara institute owner care?*

---

## 1. The product in one line

**Acharya is an exam-authentic test-paper engine.** An institute owner opens it, picks an exam, picks
a subject, picks a chapter — and gets a complete test paper in about 30 seconds, printed under
**their own institute's name and logo**. Unlimited papers. No paper-setting work.

There is more in the product (a WhatsApp tutor, a student practice app, a weak-topic dashboard). But
**test papers are the wedge.** That is what we lead with, because that is the pain owners feel every
single week.

---

## 2. Why an owner cares — the actual arithmetic

Ask any owner how long one good test paper takes. The honest answer is **2–4 hours**: finding
questions, checking the answer key, typing it, formatting it, printing it. Now multiply:

- 3 batches × 1 test a week × 3 hours = **9 hours a week gone**, every week
- The hours come out of the owner's own evenings, or out of a teacher he pays for teaching
- And when the week is busy, **the test gets skipped** — which is the thing that actually costs him
  students, because parents notice when tests stop

Our pitch is not "AI is exciting." It is: **those 9 hours come back, and the test never gets
skipped again.**

## 4. What Acharya is NOT — say these out loud in the room

Honesty here builds more trust than any feature. Owners in Bihar have been sold to before, badly.

- ❌ **It does not replace their teaching.** It is a test-paper and practice engine. Say that plainly.
- ❌ **It does not guarantee marks or results.** Never promise a result. Say *"zyada practice, aur
  progress dikhta hai"* — never *"rank aayegi."*
- ❌ **It is not used by thousands of institutes.** We are newly launched and looking for our first
  serious partners. Say exactly that. It is true, and owners respect it more than a fake crowd.
- ❌ **Combined General Studies still has ~78 questions in a mixed bucket** — generic *"which
  statement is correct?"* stems with no clean topic. If the picker surfaces one, say so. Don't hide
  a flaw the owner can see with his own eyes.
- ❌ **It is not a full ERP.** No fee collection, no attendance, no biometric. If they want that,
  they want Classplus — and that is fine, we sit alongside it.

**The test for anything you're about to say:** could the owner check it on his own phone in 60
seconds and find you wrong? Then don't say it.
