# Field Research Guide — sensing the real problems in coaching/tuition (Rohan carries this)

> Goal: from every institute/teacher visit, come back with **rich, comparable data on what's actually
> broken in how they teach** — so Deepak can decide what Acharya builds next. This is RESEARCH, not a
> pitch. The pitch only happens *after* you've found a real pain Acharya fits. Feeds
> `08_PAIN_POINT_LOG.md`. Companion to `ROHAN_FIELD_PITCH.md` (the pitch, used later).

---

## The 3 golden rules of good research (this is what makes the data rich)
1. **Ask about their PAST, not the future.** "Kal kitne doubts aaye aur kaise handle kiye?" (real) beats
   "Kya aapko AI tutor chahiye?" (useless — everyone says yes to be polite). Specifics of last week > opinions.
2. **Don't pitch. Don't sell. Just dig.** The moment you talk about Acharya, they stop telling you the
   truth and start being polite. Understand first. **Talk 20%, listen 80%.**
3. **Chase the "why" + the number.** Every pain → "kitna time/paisa/students isme jaata hai?" A pain with
   a number attached is gold; a vague complaint is noise. Ask "aur?" / "matlab?" / "phir kya hua?" 3 times.

**Write answers VERBATIM where you can** — their exact words are the data. Don't summarize into "they
want an app." Capture "raat ko 10 baje bachhe doubt bhejte हैं, main so jaata hoon, subah tak bhool jaate हैं."

## 🎯 The ONE question (if you only get 2 minutes)
> **"Padhane ke *alawa* aapka sabse zyada time kis cheez mein jaata hai — aur uske liye abhi aap kya karte हैं?"**
> *(Besides teaching, what eats most of your time — and what do you do about it today?)*

That single answer tells us the #1 pain + the current (bad) solution. Everything else is depth on top.

## SECTION 1 · Their world (who they are — 2 min)
| Ask | Why / listen for |
|---|---|
| Kya aur kis class/exam ke students padhate हैं? (NEET/JEE/boards/foundation/SSC…) | subject + exam = which concept bank |
| Abhi kitne students? Kitne batches? | size — ICP is small owner-run (10–200) |
| Aap akele padhate हैं ya aur teachers bhi हैं? | **solo teacher vs institute** — changes what they need |
| Per student fees kitni per month? | economics + willingness-to-pay proxy |
| Kitne saal se? Kaise shuru kiya? | maturity + rapport |

## SECTION 2 · A day in their life (the workflow — where the hours go)
Anchor to **yesterday / last week**, not "generally".
| Ask | Why / listen for |
|---|---|
| "Kal ka din batao — class ke alawa kya-kya karna pada?" | surfaces hidden, repetitive work |
| Students doubt kab aur kaise poochte हैं? Kal kitne aaye, kaise nipataye? | **doubt workflow + volume** (Acharya's core) |
| Ghar pe revision/practice hota hai? Aap kaise pakka karte हैं ki karein? | the revision gap (retention) |
| Test/practice papers kaun banata hai? Kitni baar? Ek paper mein kitna time? | **test-making pain — get the hours** |
| Parents ko progress kaise batate हैं? Wo kitni baar poochte हैं? | parent-communication pain |
| Attendance, fees yaad dilana, timetable — kaise manage hota hai? | admin/ops pain |

## SECTION 2b · Focus & deep-understanding probe (tests a specific solution we're building)
We think students' *focus* and *depth of understanding* is a big hidden pain. Find out if these
institutes feel it — their answers decide whether we build the "Dhyan" focus agent. Ask naturally:
| Ask | Why / listen for |
|---|---|
| "Students focus kar paate हैं padhai pe, ya dhyan bhatakta hai? Kaise pata chalta hai?" | is attention a named problem? |
| "Aisा hota hai — student ko lagta hai *samajh gaya*, par test mein galat karta hai? Kitni baar?" | **the illusion-of-understanding pain** (our sharpest wedge) |
| "Aapke students concept **deeply** samajhte हैं, ya ratta maar ke aate हैं?" | shallow vs deep processing |
| "Ghar pe baith ke focused padhai kar paate हैं, ya bikhri hui?" | deep-work structure gap |
| "Agar ek cheez aapke students ki focus ya samajh sudhaar de — wo kya hoti?" | do THEY name it unprompted? |
> Log which of these they say "haan, yehi problem hai" to — a focus/depth pain named by 3+ institutes =
> green light to build Dhyan for real.

## SECTION 3 · The pain (rank it + put a number on it)
| Ask | Why / listen for |
|---|---|
| "In sab mein sabse thaka dene wala / bekaar time-khaau kaam kaun sa hai?" | the **#1 pain, their words** |
| "Roz kitne ghante padhane ke alawa ke kaam mein chale jaate हैं?" | quantify the non-teaching load |
| "Agar ye problem gayab ho jaaye, aapko kya milega?" (time/students/paisa) | **value of solving it** |
| "Pichle mahine koi student isliye chhoda kyunki theek support nahi mila?" | **churn = the sharpest pain**; get the story |

## SECTION 4 · What they already do (existing solutions + real spend)
| Ask | Why / listen for |
|---|---|
| "In problems ke liye abhi kya use karte हैं?" (WhatsApp group / Classplus / YouTube / register / kuch nahi) | current workaround |
| "Kisi tool/app pe paisa dete हैं? Kitna, kis cheez ke liye?" | **what they PAY for = proven willingness-to-pay** |
| "Wo cheez theek kaam karti hai? Usme kya kami hai?" | the gap = our wedge |

## SECTION 5 · The dream (the job they'd outsource — points at what to build)
| Ask | Why / listen for |
|---|---|
| "Agar main aapko ek assistant doon jo roz **ek** kaam kar de — wo kaun sa kaam hoga?" | **the #1 agent to build first** |
| "Students ko kaunsi cheez sabse zyada help karegi jo abhi nahi milti?" | the student-side gap |

## SECTION 6 · AI attitude + objections (surface the No BEFORE any pitch)
| Ask | Why / listen for |
|---|---|
| "AI / ChatGPT ke baare mein kya sochte हैं? Padhane mein use karte हैं?" | open / curious / skeptical / hostile |
| "Agar ek AI aapke naam se students ke doubts WhatsApp pe solve kare — kya chinta hogi?" | **objections, unprompted** (trust, "phone pe padhai nahi hoti", cost, setup) |
| (If they tried an app before) "Pehle koi app liya tha? Kyun chhoda?" | **why tools failed for them** — the most useful "no" |

## SECTION 7 · Close the research (still not a sale)
| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| "Aur koi badi problem jo maine nahi poochi?" | open catch-all — often the best answer |
| "Aapke jaise 2–3 aur teachers jo isse struggle karte हैं?" | referrals = free warm leads |

**Only now**, if a real pain clearly matches Acharya → offer the free pilot (see `ROHAN_FIELD_PITCH.md`).
If it doesn't fit → thank them warmly and log the "no + why". **Both outcomes are a successful visit.**

## 🔎 How Deepak reads this across N institutes (turning answers → decisions)
After ~10–15 visits, look across all the capture rows for:
1. **Most frequent #1 pain** → that's the next Acharya agent to build (tests? parents? revision? admin?).
2. **What they already pay for + how much** → the real price anchor + proof of willingness-to-pay.
3. **The most common objection** → what to fix in the product or the positioning (e.g. "phone pe padhai nahi hoti" → lean on teacher-brand + parent-visible).
4. **Churn stories** → the sharpest, most emotional value prop for the pitch.
5. **Solo-teacher vs institute split** → do their top pains differ? (institutes → analytics/multi-teacher; solo → time/doubts). Shapes two offers.
6. **The gap between "dream assistant" answers and what Acharya does today** → the roadmap, ranked by how often each job is named.

A pain named **3+ times with a number attached** = build it. A pain named once = watch. This is how we
let the real problem — not our guess — decide what Acharya becomes.
