## 5. What Acharya actually does (concrete features, not marketing)

When a teacher signs up:
1. **We build a concept-bank** for their subject (their syllabus, their exam, their teaching style).
2. **We brand it** — logo + color + name (*"Kumar Sir's Acharya"*, not *"TrigunAI Tutor"*).
3. **We onboard their students** — teacher gives us the list, students get a welcome WhatsApp.
4. **Acharya answers doubts step-by-step** — never gives the answer directly; asks them to think.
5. **Sends daily practice** based on where each student is stuck (spaced repetition).
6. **Sends the teacher a weekly report** — CSV, dashboard, or a WhatsApp summary.
7. **Teacher can override anything** — if Acharya said something wrong, teacher's word wins.

**What Acharya is NOT:**
- Not ChatGPT rebranded (built on our own tutoring loop + learner profile per student).
- Not a course-selling platform (we're not competing with Byju's or Physicswala).
- Not a replacement for the teacher (it makes the teacher's classroom bigger, not obsolete).

## 13. Company values you carry into every call

1. **Honesty over hype.** We are newly launched. Say it. Teachers trust that more than "1000+ users."
2. **Teachers come first.** We are their tool, not their competitor. If Acharya makes a teacher
   feel replaceable, we've failed. Frame Acharya as "your extra hands", not "your replacement."
3. **Small hard promises, not big soft ones.** "24×7 answers" — yes. "Your students will top" — never.
4. **Language stays clean.** Use Hindi/Hinglish that matches the teacher's own. No English jargon
   ("AI-powered LLM-based personalization" — never). If they say *doubt*, you say *doubt*.
5. **We show up.** If a teacher signs the pilot, we onboard within 24-48h. No excuses.
