# 10 · The rules, and when to call Deepak

> Short file. Read it once, properly. Everything here has cost somebody something.

---

## 1. The honesty rules — never break these

These are not style guidance. Breaking them damages the company in a town where everyone knows
everyone, and they are grounds for ending the appointment.

- **Never promise marks, ranks or results.** Say *"zyada practice, aur progress dikhta hai."*
- **Never say thousands of institutes use us.** We are newly launched and looking for our first
  serious partners. Say that. It works better anyway (`06_OBJECTIONS.md` §8).
- **Never demo or describe a feature that isn't live.** If they ask for something we don't have,
  say *"abhi ye nahi hai"* and log it. **Never mock up a fake screen.**
- **Never hide a flaw the owner can see himself.** The mixed-bucket GS questions, the fact that we
  don't do fee collection — say them before he finds them.
- **Never collect cash.** Payments go only to the official TrigunAI link, sent by Deepak.
- **Never quote a price that isn't in `05_PRICING_AND_OFFER.md`.**

**The test:** could the owner check this on his own phone in 60 seconds and find you wrong? Then
don't say it.

## 2. The conduct rules

- **Never cold-WhatsApp a scraped number.** Call, or walk in. Opt-in comes from his own mouth.
  Our business number also serves live students and Rohan's Patna work — one block hurts everyone.
- **Never share the lead list, the CSV, or your login/key with anyone** — including a hire. Every
  person gets their own from Deepak, so it can be revoked when they leave.
- **Never touch our servers or the live system.** Real students are on it right now. If something
  looks broken, report it — do not try to fix it.
- **Never commit to a territory, an exclusivity, or a timeline** on the company's behalf.

## 3. Log everything, same day

No log = it did not happen. This is how you get credit, how your team gets paid, and how Deepak sees
the region without calling you every evening.

**Log the nos with their exact reason.** A no with a reason is more valuable to the company than a
maybe, and you will never remember it accurately tomorrow.

---

## 4. Call Deepak the same day when

| Situation | Why it can't wait |
|---|---|
| **An owner wants to pay now** | This is the whole point of the company. Do not let a ready buyer sit overnight. |
| **Anything broke during a demo** | Send: screenshot + which exam + which chapter. Rohan's 17 Aug bug report was fixed the same night. |
| **A price or term request outside the sheet** | Guessing costs more than a two-hour delay. |
| **An owner asks about reselling** | Serious lead, and the partner terms are being re-cut. |
| **You want to hire someone** | He mints their kit and access. |
| **Something feels wrong** — a lead who wants money up front, a demand for our data, anything odd | Trust the instinct. Ask. |

**Weekly, not daily:** your number — institutes listed, conversations, demos, **batches live**.

**Never wait to report a failure.** A pilot that died in week 2 is useful information in week 2 and
useless in week 6. Nobody here is upset by a no; people are upset by a surprise.

---

## 5. What Deepak owns, so you never have to guess

- Product, and anything about what it will do next
- All pricing and all compensation
- Any commitment made to a customer
- Access, keys, and kits for every person in the region

## 6. What you own

- The Ara list, the routes, the daily work
- Who you hire, train and let go (with his sign-off on their pay)
- The region's number
- **Correcting this kit.** Everything in it was written from outside Ara. When the ground disagrees
  with the document, **the document is wrong.** Say so, and it gets changed.
