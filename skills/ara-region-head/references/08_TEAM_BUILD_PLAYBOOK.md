# 08 · Building your Ara team

> **Do not open this in week 1.** The trigger to start hiring is **one pilot live in Ara with
> students actually taking tests** — not a promising conversation, not a verbal yes.
>
> **Why the gate matters:** you cannot train someone into a motion you have not run yourself. If you
> hire before you have closed one, you will teach them your guesses, they will fail, and you will
> lose a month plus the money. Rohan ran Patna alone for weeks before anything scaled. That order is
> deliberate.

---

## 1. What you are actually hiring for

Not "a salesman." You are hiring **feet and a notebook**: someone who can walk into a coaching
centre, get an owner talking for ten minutes, run a three-minute demo off a phone, and write down
what happened the same day.

**Hire for these four, in this order:**

1. **Shows up.** Reliability beats charm by a mile in this job. Most field hires fail on attendance,
   not on skill.
2. **Can make a stranger talk.** Test it in the interview — see §3.
3. **Writes things down.** If they can't log, they don't exist to the company.
4. **Local.** Knows the lanes, the colleges, the coaching belts, and ideally a few owners.

**Do not hire for:** English, degrees, a "sales background", or a good-looking CV. None of them
predict anything here.

---

## 2. Who to look for in Ara

| Profile | Why it works | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Recent graduate, V.K.S. University / Maharaja College** | Knows every coaching centre in town because he studied at one; hungry; cheap to start | May be preparing for an exam himself — will vanish before the exam. Ask directly. |
| **A tutor who teaches part-time** | Owners respect him, he speaks their language, he already knows the pain | Conflict of interest if he runs his own batch — check |
| **Someone who has done field sales** (SIM cards, insurance, medical rep) | Already knows how to walk into a shop and not freeze | May be a talker who never logs. Test the writing. |

**Start with ONE.** Two is not twice the output at the beginning; it is twice the confusion. Add the
second only when the first is producing without you standing next to him.

---

## 3. The interview — 30 minutes, three tests

Skip the CV conversation entirely. Run these:

**Test 1 — Can he make a stranger talk? (10 min)**
Take him to an actual coaching centre. Tell him: *"Go in and find out how they make their test
papers. Don't sell anything."* Wait outside.
→ **Pass:** he comes out with facts — who writes the papers, how long it takes, how often they test.
→ **Fail:** he comes out with "they said they're busy" or with a pitch he wasn't asked to give.

**Test 2 — Can he write it down? (5 min)**
*"Ab jo suna, wo likh ke do."*
→ **Pass:** specific, in the owner's own words.
→ **Fail:** vague, generalised, or three lines of nothing. This one is not trainable in a hurry.

**Test 3 — Will he be here in three months? (5 min)**
Ask straight: *"Aap kaunsa exam prepare kar rahe ho?"* Everyone in Ara is preparing for something.
That is fine — but you need to know the exam date, because you will lose him around it. A hire who
tells you honestly is a good sign. One who says "koi nahi" is often lying.

## 4. How to pay a field hire

**The numbers are set — same as Patna, and same as your own** (full detail in `11_YOUR_TERMS.md`):

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fixed | **₹10,000 / month** |
| Per verified, logged visit | **₹200** (cap 25/month) |
| Per converted visit — a batch goes live | **₹500** |
| Per first payment received by TrigunAI | **₹1,100** |

⚠️ **Confirm each hire's package with Deepak before you offer it.** You pick the person; he approves
the money. Never agree pay before you have his yes in writing — walking it back later costs you that
person's trust for good.

**The two rules that must hold:**
1. **Paid against verified logs only.** No log = no visit = no payment. Say this on day one, plainly,
   and never make an exception — the first exception destroys the whole system.
2. **The bonus sits on the real outcome** — a batch live and students taking tests — **not** on
   "owner was interested." Pay for what you actually want, or you will get a lot of interest and no
   installations.

## 5. How to run the week (keep it this light)

| When | What | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday morning** | Assign each person ONE area for the week + their target list from the CSV | 20 min |
| **Every evening** | They log. You read the logs. You do not need to call. | 10 min |
| **Wednesday** | Ride along with **one** of them on a real visit. Watch, don't rescue. | 2 hrs |
| **Saturday** | The week's number: visits, demos, pilots live, tests actually taken | 30 min |

**The Wednesday ride-along is the whole training system.** You will learn more about why someone is
failing in one real visit than in ten phone calls, and you cannot fix a demo you have never watched.

**Rule: watch, don't rescue.** Let them run the bad demo to the end. Debrief in the auto afterwards.
If you take over in the room, they learn nothing and lose the owner's respect permanently.

## 6. What you own vs what Deepak owns

| You (Ara) | Deepak |
|---|---|
| Who gets hired and fired | All compensation numbers |
| The list, the routes, the daily work | Product, pricing, and any commitment to a customer |
| Training and the weekly rhythm | Minting each hire's kit + SSH access (`transfer-caller-role`) |
| The region's number | Whether the region gets more people |

**When you want to add a person:** tell Deepak the name, what they'll do, and what you propose to pay
them. He mints their kit and their access. **Never share your own key or login with a hire** — every
person gets their own, so it can be revoked cleanly when they leave.

---

## 7. The failure mode to watch for

A team that produces **activity** instead of **installations**: lots of visits logged, lots of
"interested" owners, zero batches live.

The fix is not more visits. It is to look at what the team is being praised and paid for. If you
celebrate visit counts, you will get visit counts. **Celebrate — out loud, in the group — only two
things: a batch that went live, and a good honest NO with its real reason.**

That is the same trap Deepak fell into on the product side for a year, at much greater cost. Read
`01_PRODUCT_MASTER.md` §7 and don't repeat it in the field.
