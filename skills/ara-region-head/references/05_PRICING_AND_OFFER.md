The pilot is free. Money is discussed after they have used it for a month. **This is our strongest

### White-label is FREE and self-serve

Their logo, their colour, their institute's name and watermark on every printed paper — set by them
from their own dashboard at `/teacher/branding`. No charge, no waiting on us.

⚠️ **The ₹55,000 one-time white-label charge is DEAD. Never quote it.** It was replaced by the free

## 2. The objection this pricing was designed to kill

You will get **"per student kitna?"** almost every time, because that is how Classplus and every
other platform prices, and owners have been burned by it — their bill grows every time they succeed.

> **Answer: *"Per student kuch nahi. Flat hai."***

Then let the silence do the work. For an owner with 200 students who has been quoted per-head
pricing before, this is the moment the conversation changes.

- **Never collect cash. Ever.** Not "I'll deposit it tomorrow", not "the owner insisted".

**If an owner wants to pay on the spot: that is the best problem in this job. Call Deepak
immediately** — same hour, even if it's evening. Do not let a ready buyer wait overnight.

The correct response to all of these is identical: *"Sir main confirm karke bata deta hoon."*

## 6. If they want to resell it

Some owners — especially the ones who already run more than one branch — will ask whether they can
sell Acharya to other institutes and earn from it.

**Do not negotiate this in the room. Do not quote a partner rate.** There is a partner programme,
but its terms are currently being re-cut against the flat ₹150/₹250 pricing, and quoting the old
numbers would be wrong.

Your line:

> *"Haan sir, iske liye humara partner program hai. Pehle aapke apne institute pe chalate hain,
> phir main Deepak sir se aapki baat karata hoon."*

Then tell Deepak. An owner who wants to resell is a **serious** lead, not a distraction.
