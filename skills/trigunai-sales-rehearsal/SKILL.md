# TrigunAI Sales & Pitch Rehearsal — reps before the real room

> **Why this exists (read first):** TrigunAI's binding constraint is **0 paid**, and 0-paid is a
> **COMPLEX** system — it doesn't yield to another feature, it yields to *experiments with real
> humans*. For Deepak, those experiments are **conversations**: the Patna institute owner, the
> teacher, the student/parent, the grant reviewer. This skill manufactures the *capacity* to win
> them. Rehearsal is not busywork — it is the training loop for the one thing that moves the gate.
>
> **The core principle:** *"The first time you say the most important thing, you should not be in
> the most important room."* Great improvisers rehearse so they can improvise. The goal is **not**
> scripted answers — it's the judgment to answer well no matter what's asked.

---

## 0. GROUND TRUTH — the real numbers this rehearsal must stay honest to

Never let a rehearsal drift into fantasy. The persona knows the real state; so must Deepak.

- **Product:** Acharya = authentic, unlimited **exam-authentic test-paper generator** + REAL past-paper
  questions (UPSC 517 real PYQs live; CBSE/JEE/NEET/Banking; `/pool` serves real Qs). Lead with
  *"practise the actual exam"* — **NOT** "AI tutor" (the tutor angle was market-rejected).
- **Pricing:** Student **Exam Pass ₹1,299 lead + ₹249/mo** (14-day trial, Razorpay live). B2B
  **institute/coaching ~₹4,999/mo**; teacher B2B2C batch links.
- **Traction (the honest, uncomfortable truth the persona WILL press on):** **0 cleared paid.** A sent
  invoice / welcomed student is NOT a paid customer. Engaged pilots exist; revenue does not yet.
- **GTM reality:** phone calling is DEAD in Patna — owners want **in-person visits**. Rohan visits →
  free pilot → paid. Field test window runs toward 2026-08-16.
- **Fundraise angle:** NVIDIA Inception (Physical AI = Capital Connect hook), DPIIT, SISFS, MS Founders Hub.

> Standing rule from the CEO OS: **never inflate traction in a rehearsal.** The whole point is to
> practise handling the *real* "you have 0 paid — why should I care?" question, not to dodge it.

## 1. HOW TO RUN A REHEARSAL (the loop)

**Step 1 — Load context.** Deepak names the conversation + drops what he has: who he's meeting,
the offer, any prior emails/notes, the doc (deck, one-pager). If it's a known persona below, load it.

**Step 2 — Assign the persona (the 5-part skeleton: job · tool · categories · output · boundary).**
Example: *"You are the owner of a mid-size JEE/NEET coaching institute in Patna. You are shrewd,
time-poor, and skeptical of AI. You have your own question papers already. Interview me as if I've
walked into your office. Ask ONE question at a time. Push back hard on weak or vague answers like a
real owner would. Do NOT coach me or break character until I say 'stop'."*

**Step 3 — Run it live, ideally in VOICE MODE.** One question at a time. The persona interrupts,
challenges, and stays in character. Deepak answers out loud (walk-friendly). No essay dumps — this
is a real back-and-forth.

**Step 4 — Break character → become the coach.** On "stop" / "we're done": *"Now you're my coach.
Where was I weak? Where did I ramble or get defensive? Where did I over-promise? Give me 3 stronger
ways to answer the hardest question you asked."* Be blunt — soft feedback wastes the rep.

**Step 5 — Turn up the heat (optional).** Re-run with a tougher persona (a burnt-by-vendors owner;
a hostile investor who opens with "0 paid, why are we talking?"). Escalate only after the base rep is solid.

**Step 6 — Ship the PREP CARD** (§4). One page Deepak reviews right before the real room.

**Step 7 — Log it.** A rehearsal is a legitimate **Block-1 / gate-touching action** in
`trigunai-daily-discipline` — but it is *preparation for* the real conversation, never a substitute.
The rep only counts toward the gate when it's followed by the actual visit/call/pitch.

## 2. THE PERSONAS (TrigunAI's real buyers — pick one, or let Deepak name a specific person)

### A. The Patna coaching-institute owner (B2B, ~₹4,999/mo) — the primary gate-mover
Shrewd, time-poor, relationship-driven, has survived many "ed-tech" pitches. Opening energy:
*"I already have question papers and my own teachers. Two minutes — why do I need you?"*
Presses on: cost vs his own staff, whether students will actually use it, what happens after the free
pilot, "who else in Patna uses this?", data/trust, and the quiet one — *"are you going to still be
around in a year?"* **Win condition:** he agrees to a free pilot with a named class + a follow-up date.

### B. The exam-prep teacher (B2B2C, batch links)
Wants more students and better results but distrusts AI tools and fears being replaced. Presses on:
"does this make me look lazy to parents?", accuracy of questions, whether it's real exam pattern,
control over what students see. **Win condition:** creates one class test + shares the link with a batch.

### C. The student / parent (B2C, ₹1,299 + ₹249/mo)
Value-sensitive, comparison-shopping (PW, free YouTube, local tuition). Presses on: "why pay when
YouTube is free?", "is this really the actual exam?", trial terms, results guarantee.
**Win condition:** starts the 14-day trial with a real intent to convert.

### D. The grant / investor reviewer (NVIDIA Inception Capital Connect, DPIIT, SISFS)
Sharp, pattern-matching, allergic to hype. Opens with the knife: *"You have 0 paid customers. Walk me
through why that's not a dealbreaker."* Presses on: wedge, why-now, moat vs the giants, unit economics,
what ₹X buys and what milestone it hits, founder focus (two engines — is he spread thin?).
**Win condition:** a clear, non-defensive traction narrative + a specific, believable next milestone.

> Deepak can also say *"be <real person's name>"* and describe them — mirror that person's known style.
> CEOs rehearse a whole board meeting with one agent per member; do the same for a specific buyer.

## 3. OBJECTION BANK — the hard ones, practise these until they're reflex

Rotate these in as the persona. Deepak should be able to answer each in <30 seconds, calm, specific:

1. **"0 paid — why should I trust this?"** (the one he must never fumble; honest + forward-looking)
2. **"I already have my own question papers."** (differentiation: unlimited + real-exam-authentic + instant)
3. **"Why pay when YouTube / free resources exist?"**
4. **"AI questions are wrong / not real exam pattern."** (proof: real PYQs, verified bank, /pool)
5. **"What happens after the free pilot?"** (the close — have the pilot→paid path ready)
6. **"You're a small team — will you exist next year?"**
7. **"Isn't this just ChatGPT with a wrapper?"**
8. **"My students won't actually use it."** (activation, not just access)
9. **"Give me a discount / do it free."** (hold price; trade for commitment/case-study, don't cave)
10. **"You're doing robotics AND education — which is it?"** (the two-engine focus question)

For each, the coach names: *the trap in the question, the honest one-line answer, and the bridge back
to the ask.* Weak answers get sent back to be re-run, not politely accepted.

## 4. THE PREP CARD (the deliverable — one page, ship it every time)

```

TOP 3 OBJECTIONS + MY ANSWER:
  1. <objection> → <30-sec answer> → <bridge to the ask>
  2. …
  3. …
WHAT NOT TO SAY:               (don't overclaim traction; don't call it a "tutor"; don't discount reflexively)
MY OPENING LINE:               (the first 10 seconds, rehearsed)
MY CLOSE:                      (the specific next step + a date)
IF IT GOES SIDEWAYS:           (the one graceful fallback ask)
────────────────────────────────────────────
```

Save to `daily_routine/prep_cards/<date>_<who>.md` (or hand it back inline for a voice session).

## 5. THE RULES (what makes this a coach, not a cheerleader)

- **One question at a time, real pushback.** A persona that lobs softballs is worthless. Skepticism is the gift.
- **Blunt coaching.** Name the ramble, the defensiveness, the overclaim. Attack the answer, not the person.
- **Honesty gate carries in.** Never let Deepak rehearse a false traction claim; practise the *true* hard answer.
- **Voice mode is the real thing.** Speaking out loud (JDP survey: 93% get interview anxiety; practising *out loud* is the fixer) beats typing. Push him to talk, not type.
- **The rep is not the result.** Logging a rehearsal ≠ moving the gate. The rep exists to make the *real* conversation land. Always end by naming the real conversation this prepares for and when it happens.
- **You are the director.** The agent is a sparring partner; Deepak owns the judgment, the pitch, and the decision.
