**Every morning, one thing:** open Claude Code in your **Rohan-TrigunAI-Work** folder and type:

That's your switch-on. Claude then walks you through everything — you never need to remember commands.

## Your day, in one loop
1. **"start my day"** → it shows who to work in today's area + Maya's pre-call list.
2. **Maya pre-calls** the institutes (warms them, confirms a visit). *"Maya, call these: …"*
3. **You go visit** the warm ones. Understand their problem first — don't sell. *"Prep me for [institute]"* before you go.
4. **If Acharya fits their problem → put it live free** for one batch. If it doesn't fit → find out **why**.
5. **Log it the same day** — every visit + call. *"Log my visit to [institute]: …"* / *"Survey note for [institute]: said no because …"*
6. **End of day:** *"show my week"* + send Deepak one line on anything blocking you.

## What you're really doing
You're not a salesman. **You're understanding how each coaching institute actually runs and where its
real pain is** — and offering Acharya (our AI teaching assistant) when it fits. A logged **"no + the
reason"** is a WIN — it tells us what to build next. The *why* is worth more than the sale.

## The 4 rules (never break)
1. **Understand first, don't push.** Solve their problem; don't sell a product.
2. **Call/visit first — never cold-WhatsApp** a teacher before they say "yes, send it."
3. **Never promise marks/results; never say "thousands use it."** We're finding our first partners.
4. **Never collect cash.** Payment goes only to the official TrigunAI link.

**Minimum good day = 3 real conversations (visit or call) + 3 logs.** Quality of what you learn beats count.
