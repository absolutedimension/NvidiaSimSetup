# Rohan's field pitch card (carry this in your head)

> The whole philosophy: **you're not selling — you're understanding their problem and offering a
> solution.** Lead with their pain, not our product. If Acharya fits, put it live free. If it
> doesn't, log WHY (that's still a win). Never promise marks. Never say "thousands use it." Never
> take cash — payment goes only to the official TrigunAI link.

## What Acharya is (one line)
**Acharya is an AI teaching assistant** — it gives the teacher's students a 1:1 tutor on WhatsApp,
trained on how *that* teacher teaches, in their name. It **extends the teacher, never replaces them.**

## Open with DISCOVERY (ask, don't pitch)
- "Aapke padhane ke baad, sabse zyada time kis cheez mein jaata hai?" *(After class, what eats most of your time?)*
- "Students doubt kab poochte hain — aur unko jawab kaise milta hai?" *(When do students ask doubts — and how do they get answers?)*
- "Revision hota hai ghar pe? Test papers kaun banata hai? Parents ko update kaise dete ho?"
- **Listen for the fire. Let them name it.**

## The pitch (say this — Hindi/Hinglish)
> "Sir/Ma'am, main **TrigunAI** se Rohan. Hum ek **AI teaching assistant — Acharya** banaye hain.
> Aapke students ko WhatsApp pe unka apna tutor milta hai — **aapke naam se, aapke padhane ke tareeke se** —
> jo raat ko 10 baje bhi unke doubts solve karta hai, revision karwata hai. Aap padhाओ, baaki repeat
> kaam Acharya sambhaal le. **Main aapko replace nahi kar raha — aapki madad kar raha hoon.**
> Ek batch pe **free** laga ke dikhata hoon — students use karein to aage baat karenge."

## What it does today (only promise these)
1. 1:1 student AI tutor — WhatsApp + web, English/हिंदी, in the teacher's style, under their brand
2. Spaced revision — pings students to recall before they forget
3. WhatsApp channel — teacher broadcasts; students ask doubts 24/7
4. Pre-class brief — "cover X again, 6/10 shaky, these 2 slipping"

## The close (low friction)
"Ek batch pe free pilot laga dete hैं — aap dekhो students actually use karte hैं ya nahi. Setup
main karता hoon." → capture: institute, owner, phone, **subject to go live on**, batch size, WhatsApp opt-in.

## If they say NO → that's data, not failure
Log exactly WHY: too costly / students won't study on WhatsApp / don't trust AI / already have an app
(Classplus?) / no time to set up / parents won't like it. Tell Claude: *"survey note for <institute>: …"*.
A logged "no + reason" tells Deepak what to fix or build next. **The why is worth more than the sale.**
