# Acharya — the Pain-Point Match Card (Rohan presents this)

> **How to use it — this is a MIRROR, not a pitch.** After discovery, show the teacher this list and
> ask: *"Inme se kaunse aapke saath hote हैं?"* Let them recognise their OWN pain in the list — that's
> when they lean in ("haan, yehi to hota hai!"). Then the killer research question:
> *"Aur koi badi problem jo is list mein nahi hai?"* — whatever they add is what we build next.
>
> **Honesty rule:** only **List A** is live today — you can promise those. **List B** is "building /
> do you have this too?" — never promise it as done. Never promise marks. Never say "thousands use it."

---

## ✅ LIST A — problems Acharya SOLVES today (you can promise these)

Present each as *their* pain first, then the one line of what Acharya does.

| The pain (their words) | What Acharya does today |
|---|---|
| **"Class ke baad बच्चे doubt le kar अटक jaate हैं — raat ko koi jawab dene wala nahi."** | A 1:1 AI tutor on **WhatsApp**, answers doubts **any hour**, in **your teaching style**, under **your name**. |
| **"Pichhle hafte jo padhaya, agle class tak aadha bhool jaate हैं — revision hota hi nahi."** | Sends each student **spaced revision** — reminds them to recall a concept *before* they forget it. |
| **"Har बच्चे pe alag dhyan nahi de paata — kaun kamzor hai late mein pata chalta hai."** | Tracks every student + gives **you a pre-class brief**: *"cover Lenz's law again — 6/10 shaky, Ravi & Priya slipping."* You walk in knowing the room. |
| **"Students ko ek jagah reach karna, homework/announcement bhejna mushkil hai."** | A **WhatsApp channel under your brand** — broadcast to the whole class; they ask & reply from where they already are. |
| **"Ghar pe practice nahi karte — bas class mein sun ke chale jaate हैं."** | Keeps them engaged between classes with **practice + the tutor**, so learning doesn't stop when class ends. |
| **"Doosri coaching/app mein chale jaate हैं — mera apna kuch nahi jise main de sakoon."** | Everything runs under **YOUR institute's name & your method** — it **strengthens your brand**, never replaces you. |
| **"English-medium apps bachhon ko samajh nahi aate."** | Works in **Hindi or English** — students use whatever they're comfortable with. |

**The frame to say out loud:** *"Ye sab main aapke naam se, aapke padhane ke tareeke se de raha hoon —
main aapko replace nahi kar raha, aapki madad kar raha hoon. Aap padhाओ, baaki repeat kaam Acharya sambhaale."*

## 🎯 The two questions that close the research
1. **"In sab mein se kaun-kaun aapke saath hote हैं?"** → they point at their real pains (validation).
2. **"Aur koi badi problem jo is list mein *nahi* hai?"** → **the gold.** Whatever they add = a pain we
   didn't know → log it. A pain named by 3+ teachers with a number = the next thing Acharya builds.

**Then:** if List-A pains clearly matched → offer the free pilot on that exact pain ("chalo isi problem
pe ek batch pe free laga ke dikhata hoon"). If not → thank them + **log every pain they named** (List A
match, List B interest, and anything new) via *"survey note for [institute]: …"*. Both are a win.
