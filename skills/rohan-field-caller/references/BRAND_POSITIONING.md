## 0. The stance (sits above everything — Deepak, 2026-07-16)

**We do not sell. We go understand the real problem in how teaching actually happens — practically,
on the ground — and build an innovative solution to it. "Acharya" is the name that solution wears.
Every real problem we solve becomes more power in Acharya. Acharya = our agentic-AI solution to the
current learning system as it truly runs.**

What this changes:
- **The field is research, not sales.** Rohan (and Maya) go to *understand*, not to push. The
  deliverable of a visit is a real problem clearly understood — the onboarding is what happens *when*
  our solution genuinely fits that problem.
- **Acharya is a living vessel, not a fixed product.** It's the current best answer to the problems
  we've found; it keeps morphing as we find more. "AI Teaching Assistant" is the shape it takes today.
- **The honest completion of the exchange is still payment.** Not-selling ≠ not-earning. When a
  solution truly removes a real pain, the teacher paying for it is the natural close — *and it is our
  truth signal*: **if no one pays, the problem wasn't real/painful enough, or our solution didn't
  land.** So revenue isn't a target we chase by pushing; it's the **evidence that we understood the
  problem correctly.** The gate (first cleared payment) stays — reframed as proof, not as a sale.

Everything below (§1–§7) is *how we talk* once we've understood a problem and Acharya solves it. §0
is *why we're in the room at all.*

## 1. The architecture (memorise this stack)

```
TrigunAI                          ← the company
  └ "Agentic AI for education"        ← the MISSION / category we are building
      └ Acharya                          ← the PRODUCT / platform  (keep the name — earned equity)
          └ "your institute's AI Teaching Assistant"   ← what Acharya IS to the buyer
              └ agents:  doubt-solver (LIVE) · practice & test maker · revision coach ·
                         parent-connect · admin/attendance     ← the SUITE (roadmap)
```

- **TrigunAI** = company. **Acharya** = the product (Sanskrit: *the revered teacher* — on-brand, keep it).
- **"Agentic AI for education"** = the category/vision we own. Use it as altitude, not as inventory.
- **Descriptor = "AI Teaching Assistant"** (replaces "AI tutor" everywhere — see §4).

## 2. The honesty rule (the Witness — non-negotiable)

**Market the vision as *direction*; sell the one real thing as *proof*.**
Today exactly ONE agent is live (the doubt-solver/tutor), it is a **hypothesis**, and there are
**0 paying customers.** Therefore:
- ✅ Say: *"We're building the agentic AI layer for coaching institutes — starting with Acharya."*
- ❌ Never say: "a full suite of AI agents" (we have one), "thousands of teachers use it" (untrue),
  "guaranteed results/marks" (never), or anything implying Acharya **replaces** the teacher.
The suite is the roadmap we *earn* by putting agent #1 live in real institutes.

## 3. The teacher-centric spine (why we win the trust sale)

We sell to **teachers**, so the teacher is always the hero; Acharya is their **assistant/staff**,
never their replacement. A "Teaching Assistant" is a real, respected role that *supports* the
teacher and works *under* them. Every message must make the teacher more powerful, under **their**
brand, in **their** style. Anything that smells like "AI replaces the teacher" is off-brand and
kills the sale.

## 4. Words — use / avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| AI Teaching Assistant · your institute's AI TA | "tutor" (replacement fear + commodity) |
| AI teaching team / AI staff (as the suite grows) | "replaces teachers", "no teacher needed" |
| under your brand · in your style · 24/7 | "thousands of users", "#1", inflated scale |
| agentic AI for education · takes the busywork off teachers | "guaranteed results / marks / rank" |
| works while you sleep · answers doubts any hour | generic "AI assistant" with no "teaching" |

## 5. The messaging ladder (copy these, adapt tone per channel)

- **Category line (the vision):** *"Agentic AI for education."*
- **Company one-liner:** *"TrigunAI builds agentic AI for education — starting with Acharya, the AI
  teaching assistant that works 24/7 under your institute's brand."*
- **Owner pitch (Rohan / Maya, in the room / on the call):** *"Acharya is your institute's AI
  teaching assistant — it answers your students' doubts on WhatsApp at any hour, in your teaching
  style, under your name, and never takes a day off. Let's put it live free for one batch and you
  see if your students actually use it."*
- **Investor / brand altitude:** *"The agentic AI layer for India's coaching institutes."*
- **What it does, one breath:** *"Doubt-solving, practice, and revision for your students — on
  WhatsApp, in your name — so you teach and Acharya handles the repeat questions."*

## 6. How this fits the Patna field motion (they're the same idea)

The field pitch IS the vision, made concrete: Rohan asks *"what would you hire an assistant to
do?"* — Acharya already does the first job (doubts/revision) today; the rest of the answer becomes
the roadmap (the suite), discovered institute by institute. Discovery-led selling and "agentic AI
for education" are the same motion: **we solve the teacher's pains with agents; Acharya is agent #1.**
