🎤 AI Singer — Voice Recording Guide

For: the person lending their voice  ·  Time: ~30–40 min  ·  Result: a custom AI singer voice that TrigunAI owns and can use to sing any song.

Read this once fully, then record in order. Don't overthink it — a calm, clean, natural take beats a "perfect" performance. We need your natural voice, not a polished studio vocal.

✅ Part 1 — Setup (5 min)

Gear (any works): a phone, a USB mic, or a laptop mic in a quiet room. A USB mic or earphones-with-mic is best, but a modern phone in a quiet room is totally fine.

Environment — this matters most:

  • 🔇 Quiet room. No fan, AC, TV, or traffic. A small room with soft furnishings (bed, curtains, clothes) beats a big empty/tiled room with echo.
  • 🎙️ Mic ~15–20 cm (a hand-span) from your mouth — keep that distance the whole time.
  • 🚫 No effects. Turn OFF reverb, "voice changer", noise-cancellation, beautify filters. We want the raw voice.
  • 🔈 Consistent comfortable volume — not whispering, not shouting.

Settings (if your app lets you): Format WAV (preferred) or high-quality M4A/MP3; sample rate 44.1 or 48 kHz; mono is fine. Good free apps: Voice Memos (iPhone), Samsung / Easy Voice Recorder (Android), Audacity (laptop).

Quick test: record 5 sec of "testing, one two three", play back. Clear, with no hum or echo? Good. If noisy/echoey, change rooms.

✍️ Part 2 — Consent (record this line first)

Begin your recording by saying this out loud:

"My name is __________. Today's date is __________. I give TrigunAI Innovations permission to use recordings of my voice to create and use a synthetic AI singing voice."

🎵 Part 3 — What to record (~25–30 min)

Record these in order. One file or one-file-per-section both work (see naming in Part 5). Leave a 2-second silence between items. If you slip, pause and redo that line — we'll clean it up.

A. Spoken warm-up — neutral voice (~2 min)

Speak naturally, like reading the news, in BOTH Hindi and English (Hindi matters — it's how we make the singer sound natively Hindi). ~1 min each: read a paragraph or just describe your day.

B. Sustained vowels — the core of the voice (~3 min)

Most important for timbre. Hold each vowel steady ~4–5 seconds at a comfortable pitch. Do the full set three times: low, medium, high in your range.

"aaaa" … "eeee" … "iiii" … "oooo" … "uuuu"
आ … ई … ऊ … ए … ओ

C. Scales — sargam (~4 min)

Sing the sargam slowly and clearly, up then down. Do it 2–3 times at different comfortable starting pitches (one lower, one higher) to teach your full range.

सा रे ग म प ध नी सां  (up)
सां नी ध प म ग रे सा  (down)
Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Saa → Saa Ni Dha Pa Ma Ga Re Sa

D. Sung passages — Hindi (~6 min) ⭐ most important for accent

Sing slowly and clearly in any simple natural tune (it need not match a real song). Sing each line, pause, repeat once. Gentle feeling.

सुर में बहता ये मन मेरा
हर साँस में एक नया सवेरा
आवाज़ मेरी है खुली हवा
गाता रहूँ मैं सुबह से शाम

Then sing these single words on a held note (clear pronunciation):

प्रेम · विरह · सपना · चाँदनी · ख़ुशी · आँसू · उम्मीद · ज़िंदगी

E. Sung passages — English (~4 min)

Same idea — simple gentle tune, slow and clear:

Here I am, my voice is free
Singing soft and easily
Morning light and evening calm
Every note becomes a song

F. Dynamics & emotion (~3 min)

Sing any one line above (Hindi or English) four ways, so the AI learns your range:

  • Soft & gentle (lullaby)
  • Bright & happy
  • Sad & longing
  • Strong & full (louder, confident)

📋 Part 4 — Do's & Don'ts

✅ Do🚫 Don't
Record in a quiet, soft roomRecord near a fan / AC / window / traffic
Keep a steady distance from the micMove closer / farther while recording
Sing & speak slowly and clearlyRush or mumble
Keep a consistent comfortable volumeWhisper one line, belt the next
Turn OFF all filters/effectsUse reverb, autotune, "beautify"
Pause 2 sec & redo if you slipAbandon the session over one slip
Drink water, stay relaxedStrain or push your voice

Target: ~20–30 minutes of clean audio. Quality > quantity — even 15 clean minutes makes a great singer.

📦 Part 5 — Naming & sending

  • One file: singer_<yourname>.wav
  • Separate files: <yourname>_A_warmup.wav, _B_vowels, _C_sargam, _D_hindi, _E_english, _F_emotion
  • Send the original files via Google Drive / WeTransfer / AirDrop. Avoid WhatsApp "audio" (heavy compression) — use WhatsApp "Document" if you must, or better, Drive.
