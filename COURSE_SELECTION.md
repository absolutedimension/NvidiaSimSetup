## RECOMMENDATION: Launch Course 4 (VR/MR App Development)

### Why this one first:

| Factor | Course 4 (VR/MR) | Course 3 (Agentic AI) | Course 1 (Robotics) | Course 2 (ML Math) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Competition intensity** | Low (~10 courses) | Very High (89K+ students on leaders) | Medium | Extreme (Coursera giants) |
| **Deepak's credibility** | ★★★★★ (shipped app) | ★★★ (built internal tools) | ★★★ (lab experience) | ★★ (practitioner, not professor) |
| **Can demo own work** | ✅ Real shipped app | Partially | Partially | No |
| **Market demand** | 64,957 open jobs, talent shortage | Hot but saturated | Steady | Saturated |
| **Content shelf life** | 2+ years (Quest platform stable) | 3–6 months (tools change fast) | 1–2 years | 3+ years |
| **Differentiation** | "I shipped this, here's how" | Hard to stand out | Physical hardware angle | Very hard |
| **Price ceiling** | Higher (professional skill) | Medium | Medium | Low (free alternatives) |

### Proposed unique angle for Course 4:

**Title idea:** *"Build & Ship Your First VR/MR App — From Unity Zero to Meta Quest Store"*

**What makes it different from the existing 10 courses:**
1. **Full pipeline, not just features.** Most courses stop at "here's how hands work." You teach the WHOLE journey: install Unity → Meta SDK setup → build scenes → test on Quest → submit to Meta → get into alpha → real users.
2. **Taught from a shipped app.** You built EnergyField — Inner Journey. You can screen-share your actual app, your actual Meta developer dashboard, your actual alpha submission.
3. **MR (Mixed Reality) focus.** Quest 3 passthrough MR is the newest frontier. Most courses are still VR-only.
4. **Real bugs, real solutions.** Not "here's the documentation." Instead: "here's the 30 things that went wrong and how I fixed each one."
5. **VR live classes delivered IN VR.** Your students learn VR dev while INSIDE VR. Nobody else does this.

### Course structure (8 modules, ~10–12 hours total):

| Module | Topic | What student builds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Setup & First Scene** — Unity install, Meta XR SDK, project config, Quest Link | Empty room running on Quest |
| 2 | **Hands & Controllers** — Input system, grab mechanics, teleportation | Interactive objects you can grab and throw |
| 3 | **UI in VR** — World-space canvas, laser pointer, menus | A working VR menu system |
| 4 | **MR & Passthrough** — Scene API, spatial anchors, room mesh | AR objects placed in your real room |
| 5 | **Physics & Interaction** — Rigidbody, colliders, haptics, sound | A mini physics game |
| 6 | **Multiplayer Basics** — Photon/Normcore, avatars, voice chat | 2-player VR room |
| 7 | **Polish & Performance** — Optimization for Quest, profiling, LOD, shaders | App running at 72fps |
| 8 | **Ship It** — Meta developer portal, build signing, App Lab/Horizon Store submission, alpha testing | App submitted to Meta |

### Pricing strategy:

| Tier | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Udemy (recorded only)** | ₹499–799 (Udemy sale price: ₹449–479) | All 8 modules, recorded |
| **Direct (recorded + VR live classes)** | ₹2,999–4,999 | Same content + weekly live VR Q&A sessions |
| **Premium (with Quest headset)** | ₹29,999–34,999 | Everything above + Meta Quest 3 shipped to student |

Start with Udemy tier. Add direct tier once VR classroom is built. Premium tier only after demand is proven.
