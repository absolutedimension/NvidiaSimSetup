> A dedicated campaign inside `MARKETING_PLAN_JULY18.md`, targeting engineering students.
> Owner: Deepak (solo) · Created 2026-06-18 · Decisions on record: **₹35k held (repositioned)** ·
> **student-direct now, colleges Q3.**

---

## 0. The insight

Every Indian B.Tech student has a **forced, urgent need**: a final-year / major **project**, a
mandatory **internship / industrial training**, and a **placement edge**. Most get garbage — bought
projects, hollow internship PDFs. We offer a *genuinely real* one: **build & ship a VR/MR app with
industry-grade tooling and an AI coding partner, submit it as your project, and earn a real
training/internship certificate.** This hits **Persona B (Future-Proofer / fresher)** dead-on.

**Timing:** mid-June–July is semester break → bad for approaching colleges (decision-makers away),
but *perfect* for students hunting summer training/internships. So: **student-direct = July-18 play;
institutional/college = Q3 play** (build the asset now, pitch in August).

---

## 1. The offer (repositioned — same ₹35k, reframed for a student)

**"8-Week VR Developer Internship + Live Project"** — what the student walks out with:

| Deliverable | Why it matters to a student |
|---|---|
| A **real, shipped VR/MR app** | Their major/final-year project — demoable on a real headset path, not a toy |
| **Live weekly mentorship** with a founder who has an app **live on the Meta Quest store** | Real guidance, not a recorded dump |
| **Industry-grade tooling** (GPU / Isaac / Unity / AI coding partner) | Genuine industry exposure — the thing internships are supposed to give |
| **Training / internship completion certificate + project letter** | Submittable for the mandatory internship/training requirement |
| **Build-with-AI skill** | Future-proof; the differentiator on a resume |
| **Portfolio piece + placement talking point** | Most peers can't show a shipped VR app |

### Making ₹35k digestible WITHOUT discounting
- **Installments** (e.g., 2–3 payments) — the single biggest lever for a student/parent.
- **Parent-facing framing** — parents fund internships/training; give them a one-pager that speaks
  outcomes (project + certificate + skill + placement edge), not features.
- **Free class first** — proof before price. Nobody commits ₹35k cold; they commit after the free class.
- **Early-bird seat** for the first cohort.

## 2. HONESTY GUARDRAILS (CEO OS — do not cross)

- ✅ "A real **training / internship-style completion certificate**" + "**project completion letter**"
  for genuine work done. This is standard and legitimate.
- ❌ NOT "AICTE-approved", NOT "university credit", NOT "your college WILL accept it" (the TPO decides),
  NOT "guaranteed placement", NOT "industry-recognized."
- ✅ "Industry-grade tools + a real live project" — true (Deepak uses exactly these).
- Frame: *"a real project + a genuine internship-style certificate you can submit."* Strong and honest.
  Overclaiming breaks trust with the exact parents who'd pay.
