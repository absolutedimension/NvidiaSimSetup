## C. If they hesitate (this IS the signal — learn from it)

Don't discount on reflex. Ask one question and listen:

> Totally fair — what's the hesitation? Is it the timing, the price, or not sure it'll work for *your* kind of work?

- **Timing** → offer to hold their seat for the next cohort, take a small deposit transfer now.
- **Price** → name the value (live debugging of *their* code + provided compute + a shipped agent). Hold the price for Cohort 1; a founding-cohort rate is fine *if you decide it*, not as a reflex.
- **"Will it work for my work?"** → this is the easiest yes. Spend 10 min mapping their workflow to the Ops Agent on a quick call. That call closes it.

> Whatever they say, **write it down.** Three honest objections, 30 days before launch, are worth more than three silent signups.
