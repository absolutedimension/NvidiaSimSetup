# Competitive Analysis — Indian Exam-Prep / Assessment Market

> **For:** positioning Acharya for Students (AI-generated adaptive tests + SWOT analytics + skill tracking, ₹199/mo, free → 14-day → paid).
> **Date:** 2026-07-23 · **Method:** deep-research (26 sources fetched, 117 claims extracted, 25 adversarially verified → 20 confirmed / 5 refuted).
> **Confidence note:** all competitor prices/features are from vendors' own pages — they prove a feature is *offered* and a price is *advertised*, not that the AI works or that a discount is permanent. Mock-test counts (150k+, 4M+ questions) are self-reported marketing aggregates.

---

## 1. The market splits into TWO archetypes (this is the key insight)

| | **JEE/NEET (coaching brands)** | **Govt exams (test-series-first)** |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Per-exam **annual** test-series bundle | Single **all-access** subscription (600–1,000+ exams) |
| Price | **₹5,000–8,000/yr** per exam cycle (PW Real Test Pass) | **~₹300–400** discounted (Adda247 Test Prime, Testbook Pass) |
| Free/monthly tier | ❌ None | ❌ No monthly; heavily discounted annual |
| "AI" depth | Conventional — rank compare, accuracy/speed reports | Conventional — topper compare, percentile, 4-band weak-area filters |
| Who | Physics Wallah, Allen, Aakash, Unacademy, Vedantu | Testbook, Adda247, Oliveboard, PracticeMock |

**Neither archetype is genuinely AI-native.** "AI-based analysis" across the Govt segment (verified on Oliveboard) is descriptive statistics on completed mocks — no AI-generated questions, no per-item adaptive difficulty.

## 3. Pricing reality check — where ₹199/mo sits

- **₹199/mo = ~₹2,400/yr.**
- **vs JEE/NEET serious bundles** (PW Real Test Pass ₹5,000–8,000/yr): **you're cheap** (~half to a third).
- **vs test-series-ONLY annual plans** (less-verified but consistent across sources: Career Launcher ₹650, Testbook ₹799, Vedantu ₹900, Allen ₹1,000, Embibe ₹1,100, Aakash ₹1,200 — all *per year*): **you're PREMIUM.** ₹2,400/yr is 2–4× these. ⚠️ **This is the pricing trap to watch.**
- **vs Govt all-access** (Adda247 Test Prime ~₹300–400 for 150k+ mocks): **you look expensive per-mock** — but that's Govt-exam-focused and shallow-AI, a different buyer.

**Takeaway on price:** ₹199/**month** reads as premium against an ₹800/**year** test-series habit. The monthly framing only wins if the buyer believes they're getting something the annual test-series can't give — **genuinely adaptive, AI-generated, personalized SWOT** — not "another mock bank." Your pricing has to be *sold on the AI*, or reconsidered (annual option? lower entry?).

---

## 4. THE WHITE SPACE (what nobody owns) — *medium confidence*

> **A consumer-priced (~₹199/mo), monthly-billed, genuinely AI-native adaptive-assessment product, direct-to-student, spanning boards + entrance, with explicit SWOT / weak-area reports.**

The intersection is empty because:
- Premium JEE/NEET = annual-only, ₹5,000+, no monthly, conventional analytics.
- Cheap Govt all-access = shallow AI, govt-exam-focused.
- True AI-native = Embibe (free, content-bundled) or Cerebry (B2B-to-schools).

Nobody sells *cheap + monthly + direct-to-student + genuinely AI-adaptive + explicit SWOT + boards-and-entrance* as one product.

**Why only medium confidence:** the research did NOT directly verify test-series pricing for Allen, Aakash, Unacademy, Vedantu, Toppr, BYJU'S Exam Prep, Extramarks, PracticeMock — the **Boards segment especially is under-covered**. A niche competitor at this exact intersection can't be fully ruled out.

---

## 5. Biggest threats to a small AI-assessment startup

1. **Embibe** — already ships your exact feature set, backed by Reliance, and it's *free*. If they launch a cheap paid tier or you can't clearly out-UX them, they're the wall. Your edge must be **distribution + focus + SWOT UX**, not raw features.
2. **Price anchoring by Govt all-access** (Testbook/Adda247 ~₹300–400 for 150k+ mocks) — makes any per-mock value proposition hard to defend on price. Don't compete on mock count.
3. **Coaching brands' bundled test series** (PW etc.) — cross-subsidize assessment with course revenue and own aspirant trust + distribution.

---

## 6. So-what for TrigunAI (the moves this implies)

1. **Do NOT sell on mock-count or price** — you lose both. Sell on **"AI that finds *your* weak spots and generates *your* next test"** — the adaptive + SWOT story Embibe proves buyers want and the Govt/coaching players fake.
2. **The AI must be visibly real** — AI-*generated* questions + *adaptive* difficulty + a personalized SWOT report the student couldn't get from a static mock bank. That's the only thing justifying ₹199/mo over an ₹800/yr test series.
3. **Reconsider the price frame** — ₹199/mo vs ₹800/yr test-series is a hard sell. Consider surfacing an **annual price** (anchor ~₹999–1,499/yr to sit between test-series and Embibe), keep monthly for flexibility. Test it.
4. **Your unfair advantage is distribution, not tech** — WhatsApp-native delivery + teacher-brand channel is something Embibe (app-first) and Cerebry (B2B) don't own. Lean into it.
5. **Under-covered = opportunity + risk** — the Boards (Class 9-12) segment had zero verified competitors. Worth a focused follow-up scan before betting the funnel there.
