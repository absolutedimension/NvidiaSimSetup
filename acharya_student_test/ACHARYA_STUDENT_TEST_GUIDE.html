Acharya — Student Exam-Prep Test Guide

A step-by-step walkthrough to test the whole student experience end-to-end, and note what works and what needs fixing.
Tester: Rohan Test on: acharya.trigunai.com Time needed: ~20–25 min Date: ____________
What you're testing: Acharya's self-serve exam-prep product for students — they take a challenge, earn free days, sign in, and practise adaptive test papers (real IIT-JEE Physics style) that find their weak topics.

हिंदी: आप एक छात्र की तरह पूरा सफ़र टेस्ट कर रहे हैं — challenge दो, free days जीतो, login करो, और अपने कमज़ोर टॉपिक पर टेस्ट दो। हर step पर लिखो क्या सही चला और क्या नहीं।

Before you start:

• Use a phone (that's how students will use it) + a Chrome/Safari browser.

• Open in a private / incognito window so you test as a brand-new student.

• Have a Gmail account ready (to test Google sign-in). If Google fails, use the email option and note it.

• For each step: tick ✅ Works or ✗ Problem, and write a note if anything felt off.

💡 How to give the most useful feedback: pretend you are a 17-year-old student preparing for JEE/NEET. Would you trust this? Would you pay ₹199/month? Note anything confusing, slow, or that looks broken — even small things.
PART A — First impression (the front page)
1Open the front page
Do: In a private window, go to acharya.trigunai.com
Expect: A page asking "Are you a student, or a teacher?" with two cards — a Student card and a Teacher card.
✅ Works ✗ Problem
2Enter the student side
Do: Under the Student card, tap "Take the challenge →"
Expect: The "Acharya Challenge — Win up to 60 days free" page opens.
✅ Works ✗ Problem
PART B — The challenge (earn free days)
3Pick a topic & start
Do: In "What do you want to be tested on?", type JEE Physics (or tap a suggestion), then tap "Start the challenge →"
Expect: After a few seconds, an 11-question test starts with a ~5-minute timer.
✅ Works ✗ Problem
4Answer the questions
Do: Answer all 11 (get some right on purpose). Reach the end.
Expect: A result: "🎉 You earned X days FREE" + your score (e.g. 6/11).
✅ Works ✗ Problem
Note the questions: were they clear? sensible? any that looked wrong? →
PART C — Sign in & claim (the important one)
5Sign in with Google
Do: On the result screen, tap "Continue with Google" and pick your Gmail.
Expect: No error — you sign in and land on your dashboard ("My Acharya").
✅ Works ✗ Problem
⚠️ If you see "Access blocked" or any Google error — STOP, screenshot it, and use the email box instead (type your email → "Claim my free access"). Note the exact error below.
6Check the dashboard
Do: Look at your dashboard.
Expect: "X free days left" (the days you earned), a "My topics" list with the topic you chose, an "Add a topic" box, and a "₹199/mo" plan card at the bottom.
✅ Works ✗ Problem
PART D — Add a subject & open its detail
7Add IIT JEE Physics
Do: In "Add a topic", type IIT JEE Physics and tap Add.
Expect: It appears in "My topics" (you can have up to 5).
✅ Works ✗ Problem
8Open the subject
Do: On the "IIT JEE Physics" topic, tap "Take test →"
Expect: A page listing chapters (Kinematics, Modern Physics, Electrostatics, …), each with subtopics you can tick, plus "Full subject test", difficulty, and number of questions.
✅ Works ✗ Problem
PART E — Build & take a real test (the heart of it)
9Build a test
Do: Tick one or two subtopics (e.g. Kinematics → Projectile Motion). Choose difficulty + 5 questions. Tap "Create test →".
Expect: A "writing your questions…" message, then after ~1–2 minutes the questions appear (real GPT-5 writing, so it is slow on purpose).
✅ Works ✗ Problem
ℹ️ If a test ever fails to appear, just tap Create test again — it is a slow AI call and occasionally times out. Only note it if it fails twice in a row.
How long did it take to appear? ______ (1–2 min is expected). Too slow? →
10⭐ Check the maths renders correctly
Do: Read the questions carefully.
Expect: Equations, fractions and units look like proper maths (e.g. clean symbols) — NOT raw code like $\frac{a}{b}$ or \text{nm}.
✅ Maths looks right ✗ Some looked like code
This is the most important thing to check. If ANY question shows raw code / broken symbols, screenshot it and note which chapter.
11Finish & see the result
Do: Answer all questions. Reach the end. Tap "See the answers".
Expect: A score, your weak vs strong topics, and worked solutions for each question.
✅ Works ✗ Problem
ℹ️ You may get fewer questions than you asked for (e.g. 3 of 5). That's normal — Acharya throws away any question too similar to a real past paper. Note it only if it happens every time.
Were the questions good quality — like a real JEE paper? →
12⭐ Check the diagrams
Do: Across the tests you take, watch for questions that include a drawn diagram (a circuit, a lens/ray sketch, a capacitor with plates). Try chapters like Ray Optics, Current Electricity or Electrostatics — they use figures most.
Expect: Where a question needs a figure, it appears as a clean picture above the question text — clear lines and labels, not blank or garbled.
✅ Diagrams look clear ✗ Blank / garbled — saw none
Screenshot any diagram that looks wrong and note the chapter. (Diagrams are a new feature — your eyes here are very useful.)
PART F — Progress & "what to practise next"
13Check your progress shows up
Do: Tap "My dashboard", then open "IIT JEE Physics → Take test" again.
Expect: Now it shows "1 test taken", small colour dots on subtopics (red/orange/green = weak→strong), and a banner "🎯 Acharya suggests: practise …" (your weakest topic).
✅ Works ✗ Problem
14Use the suggestion
Do: Tap "Practise it →" in the suggestion banner.
Expect: That subtopic gets auto-selected — you can create another test on your weak area in one tap.
✅ Works ✗ Problem
