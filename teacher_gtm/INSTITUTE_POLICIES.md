*Drafted 2026-08-01. Owner: Deepak. For B2B custom/white-label institute customers (e.g. Study Hub).
Purpose: walk into a paid deal with clear, FAIR, trust-building terms — not scary legalese, not
vague promises. Two of these are Deepak's call (marked ⚑) — confirm before quoting.*

---

## The model that makes policy easy: **pilot before pay**

The institute runs a **live pilot first** (e.g. Study Hub's Chemistry test) and *sees it work on
their own real test* before paying the ₹25,000 start fee. This is the single most important policy
choice — it removes almost all refund risk, because nobody pays until they've seen value. Keep it.

## 2. Refund & cancellation ⚑

**Start / activation fee (₹25,000):** covers real setup work (their instance, syllabus tuning, brand
activation). Because the institute has already seen a working pilot before paying:
- **7-day go-live guarantee** — if we fail to deliver a working, branded app within **2 working days**
  (a delay caused by us, not by pending content from them), the start fee is **fully refunded**.
- Once the branded app is delivered and accepted, the start fee is **non-refundable** (the setup work
  is done and specific to them).

**Monthly subscription:**
- **No lock-in. Cancel anytime** with **15 days' notice** before the next billing date.
- Cancel mid-month → service runs to the end of the paid month; **no pro-rata refund** of the current month.
- A month already paid is not refunded, but they keep access for that full month.

## 3. Data ownership & privacy (this builds trust — say it plainly)

- **The institute owns its data.** Student rosters, results, and any content they hand over remain
  **theirs**. We process it only to run their platform.
- **We never resell or share** their students' data, and never poach their students to our own brand.
- On exit, the institute can request an **export of their data**; we then **delete it within 30 days**.
- We keep student data secure and use it only to operate and improve *their* service.

## 4. Content & IP

- **Their content stays theirs.** Material they hand over for tuning is used only for their instance.
- **Our platform stays ours.** The Acharya engine, question bank, and technology remain TrigunAI's IP;
  the institute gets a licence to use it under their brand for the subscription term — not ownership
  of the underlying system.
- Test papers generated for the institute are theirs to use with their students.

## 5. Service, support & uptime

- **Support:** WhatsApp / phone support on working days; we aim to respond within one working day.
- **Uptime:** we target reliable availability; occasional maintenance windows will be notified in advance.
- **Tuning:** initial syllabus tuning is included in the start fee. Ongoing major re-tuning to new
  syllabi/subjects may be scoped separately.
- This is a **best-effort service commitment**, not a hard SLA with penalties (keep it that way until
  we're bigger).

## 6. Term & termination

- **Month-to-month** unless on annual prepay.
- Either side may end with **15 days' notice**. On termination: their branded app is deactivated at
  the end of the paid period, and data is exported/deleted per §3.
- We may suspend service for non-payment after a reminder + grace period.
