## The 3 objections that killed every visit (memorize these)

### 1. "We already have our own question banks." (Bansal, Vidya, P. Singh, Study Hub)
The established institute does **not** feel testing as its gap. This is the #1 killer.
- **Kill it by QUALIFYING IT OUT before you walk in** (see filter below). Don't spend a visit
  discovering it. If in the room it surfaces: *"Of course you do — every good institute does.
  The question isn't do you have questions, it's how many HOURS a week your teachers spend
  making fresh, exam-authentic papers by hand — and whether those are as close to the real
  JEE/NEET/board paper as ours. May I show you a paper generated in 30 seconds?"* Move from
  "do you have a bank" (they win) to "how much teacher time does your bank cost you" (we win).

### 2. "We want teaching material (PPTs / 3D visuals), not more tests." (Vidya, AIMS, Derote)
Institutes feel the *teaching* pain more than the *testing* pain. Acharya is assessment-only today.
- **Don't oversell what we don't have.** Say plainly: *"Today we're the sharpest at one thing —
  unlimited exam-authentic test papers + auto-marking + weak-topic reports, so your teachers get
  back the hours they lose making papers. Teaching aids aren't what we do yet."* Then requalify:
  *"Is making test papers a real time-cost for your teachers, or not really?"* If not → **it's
  not a lead, mark Not Needed Now and leave fast.** Stop trying to convert teaching-first institutes.

### 3. Price / build-vs-buy on the custom tier. (AIMS ₹55k; Study Hub → build own)
- ₹55k is **dead** — already dropped to **free-to-start / pay after month 1** (junior ₹150,
  senior ₹250). Lead with that, never with a big one-time number.
- **Build-vs-buy counter (say it out loud when they hint at building their own):**
  *"You can copy the screen in a weekend. What you can't copy is the 46,000 verified,
  answer-keyed questions behind it, the engine that generates a new exam-authentic paper on
  demand, and the cost of keeping that current every year. That's ₹-lakhs and months of a team
  you'd have to hire and manage. We already run it — you rent it for ₹150 a student and start free.
  Building it yourself is the expensive option."*

## QUALIFY BEFORE YOU WALK IN (saves Rohan's legs)

The 10 visits give a clean filter. **Don't visit an institute until it passes.**

### 🎯 HUNT these (they lack a bank / feel the paper-making pain)
- **Smaller / newer owner-run centres** (~10–100 students) that **can't afford a content team.**
- Institutes where **teachers make papers by hand** (AIMS teacher validated this exact pain).
- **Board-focused tuition** without a testing product (board students need volume practice).
- Ask the receptionist ONE screening question before asking for the owner:
  *"Do your teachers make their own test papers, or do you buy them ready-made?"*
  → "make their own" = **stay, pitch the time-saving.** "we're fine / have our own" = **leave.**

### 🚫 SKIP these (proven 0-conversion in the first 10)
- Established names with their **own question banks** (Bansal, big chains).
- **Teaching-first** centres that want visual/3D aids, not tests (Vidya, Derote).
- Anyone where the **decision-maker isn't in** — don't pitch office staff (P. Singh, the unnamed
  Kurji centre). Get the owner's slot first, then go.
- Stale Google listings — **call to confirm it exists before walking** (Top Tutorial didn't).

## ⭐ THE HEADLINE PLAY — sell the TEACHER, the teacher brings the students

**Market truth (founder, 08-07):** students in this market don't self-study — they study *through*
a mentor/teacher. So direct-to-student is structurally weak (no self-study demand), and **the
teacher is not just the buyer — the teacher is the distribution channel to a whole batch of
students.** One teacher adopting = their entire class on Acharya. That is leverage
direct-to-student never has.

This makes the ICP razor-sharp. The target is the teacher/small-owner who is BOTH:
- **(a) feeling the pain** — makes test papers by hand, loses hours (AIMS's teacher = the one
  validated pain in all 10 visits), AND
- **(b) carrying distribution** — has students who follow *them*, so adoption flows downstream.

We were closest to a win (AIMS) with the person we weren't actually selling to. Fix that.

**The pitch (teacher-first, not owner-budget-first):**
*"Get your evenings back — Acharya makes the exam-authentic paper in 30 seconds and auto-marks it.
Put your students on it and you see exactly where each one is weak. Free to start."*
The teacher adopts for their **own time-saving**; the students come **because they follow the
teacher** — no self-study demand required. Let the relieved teacher sell the owner from the inside.

> Skip the owner who already bought a bank (feels no pain). Find the teacher drowning in
> paper-making who has students of their own. That teacher is worth more than the institute owner.
