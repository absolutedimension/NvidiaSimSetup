# Teacher Conversation Script — discovery first, pitch second

> Rule: **first 10 minutes are THEM talking.** You are diagnosing their tuition business.
> The pitch (01_OFFER_ONE_PAGER.md) comes only after you can repeat their problem back
> to them in their own words. A pitch without discovery is a pamphlet with a voice.

## Opening (30 sec)

"Namaste [name] — I run TrigunAI; we build an AI tutor that works under a teacher's own
name on WhatsApp. Before I explain anything, I want to understand how your classes run —
can I ask a few questions? Takes 10 minutes."

## Discovery questions (ask, then be quiet; write answers verbatim)

**Their business:**
1. How many students right now? Which classes/exams? What's the monthly fee?
2. How do new students find you? What made the last student join? The last one leave?
3. What would growing to [2×] look like — what stops that today?

**The pain (listen for the one that makes them talk fast):**
4. What happens when a student has a doubt at 9 pm?
5. How much of your evening goes to doubt messages?
6. How do you know which student is quietly falling behind before the test result?
7. Have you lost students to bigger apps/coaching (Physics Wallah, BYJU'S-type, local)? Why?

**Money & authority:**
8. Do you spend anything today on tools/marketing/materials? How much?
9. If something kept 2 extra students enrolled per month, what's that worth to you?
   (Their answer prices the offer for them — 2 × fee vs ₹4,999.)

## Qualify — pitch ONLY if 3 of 4 are true

- [ ] ≥10 current paying students
- [ ] Teaches an exam-prep subject (board/NEET/govt-exam)
- [ ] Named a real pain in Q4–Q7 (doubts load / retention / competition)
- [ ] They own the money decision (it's their tuition, not an institute's)

If not qualified: thank them, ask "who do you know who runs a bigger batch?" (referral),
log it, move on. **Do not pitch unqualified — a polite 'no' from the wrong person teaches nothing.**

## Pitch → close

Deliver 01_OFFER_ONE_PAGER.md, anchored to THEIR words:
"You said [their pain, verbatim]. Here's exactly what that looks like fixed…"
→ Live demo on your phone: message Acharya a doubt from their subject, show the answer.
→ Close on the free pilot; book the onboarding time before the call ends.

## After EVERY call (5 min, non-negotiable)

Log in 03_CONVERSATION_LOG.md same day:
- outcome (pilot booked / follow-up dated / no / unqualified-referral)
- their pain, verbatim (one line)
- their objection, verbatim (one line) — this is the test's real output

## Sourcing the 10/week

1. The 2 inbound teacher leads (verify the test-number one) — TODAY.
2. Your own network: anyone who teaches or whose family runs tuition. Warm beats cold 10:1
   (the college cold blast bounced; the 3 cohort students came from warm).
3. Ask every conversation for 1 referral: "who runs the biggest batch you know?"
4. Local: tuition centres within reach — walk in with the printed pamphlet
   (`acharya_pamphlet/Acharya_Pamphlet_Teacher_A4.pdf`).
5. Teacher groups on WhatsApp/Telegram/Facebook — offer the free pilot, not the price.
