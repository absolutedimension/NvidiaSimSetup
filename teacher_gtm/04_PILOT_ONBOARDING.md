# Pilot Onboarding — what happens when a teacher says YES

> Goal: from "yes" to **first student message answered** within 48 hours. Speed here IS
> the product experience. A pilot that takes a week to start is a pilot that dies.

## Same day as the "yes" (you, ~1 hr)

- [ ] WhatsApp the teacher: pilot confirmation + what happens next (template below).
- [ ] Configure Acharya for their subject/exam/class (concept bank — use skill
      `add-trigunai-course` if the subject isn't covered yet; exam-prep subjects may
      need a new concept bank — budget 2–4 hrs the first time, reusable after).
- [ ] Set the teacher's name in Acharya's intro ("I'm [Teacher]'s tutor…").
- [ ] Send the teacher their student-invite message (template below) + the pamphlet PDF.
- [ ] Create their row in 06_PILOT_TRACKER (bottom of this file).

## Day 1–2 (teacher's job, you chase)

- [ ] Teacher forwards the invite to 10 chosen students (mix: 3 strong / 4 middle / 3 weak).
- [ ] ≥5 students send their first message to Acharya. **If <5 by day 2, call the teacher —
      the pilot hasn't started; find the blocker (wrong number saved? fear? Hindi/English?).**
- [ ] You verify in the bridge logs each student got a real, correct, on-syllabus answer.

## Day 3–14 (the pilot proper)

- [ ] Daily: check delivery + answer quality in bridge logs (5 min). Any wrong/weird answer:
      fix the concept bank same day and tell the teacher you fixed it (this builds trust).
- [ ] Day 7: send the teacher the first **weekly progress report** (who practiced, who's
      stuck, top weak topics). This report is the thing they'll pay for — make it good.
- [ ] Day 10: mid-pilot call with the teacher: "what are students saying?" Log verbatim.

## Day 14 — the close

- [ ] Send report #2 + the close message (template below) with the **Razorpay link
      (₹4,999/mo subscription)**.
- [ ] On payment cleared: welcome note + expand from 10 → their full batch (≤50 active).
- [ ] On "no": one question — "what would have made it a yes?" Verbatim → 03_CONVERSATION_LOG.md.

## Templates

**Pilot confirmation (to teacher, WhatsApp):**
"[Name] ji, great — your 14-day free pilot starts now. I'm setting up Acharya for
[subject/class] under your name today. Tomorrow I'll send you one message to forward to
10 students — that's all you have to do. You'll get a progress report every week. After
14 days you decide: ₹4,999/month, or we stop, no charge."

**Student invite (teacher forwards):**
"📚 I've arranged a personal tutor for you — on WhatsApp, free. Save this number: [number].
Send it any doubt from [subject], any time — it will solve it step by step and give you
daily practice for [exam]. Start now: just message 'Hi [Teacher name]'s tutor'."

**Day-14 close (to teacher):**
"[Name] ji — two weeks done. [X] of your students practiced [Y] times; report attached.
To keep Acharya running for your batch (up to 50 students): ₹4,999/month — [Razorpay link].
If it's a no, it stops tonight, no charge — and I'd genuinely value one line on why."
