## WHY private tutors (not institutes) — the strategic basis

Field data (Aug 7, `FIELD_DATA_READOUT_10VISITS.md`): 10 institute visits → **0 converting.** The only
validated pain was *the teacher who makes papers by hand* — but at an institute that person doesn't hold
the budget. A **private tuition teacher collapses pain-holder + budget-holder into one person**, has no
question bank, and no dev team → **build-vs-buy (what killed Study Hub) never arises.** They can say yes
on the spot.

## THE MODEL — DUAL TRACK (decided 2026-08-11)

Two ways a private tutor becomes revenue. Run **both**; SELL is the gate play (fastest to first ₹).

**Track 1 — SELL (primary / gate):** tutor pays for the tool to serve their own students
(free-to-start → ₹150 junior / ₹250 senior per month). One clean decision-maker; the person who
feels the pain holds the budget. Shortest path to the first cleared ₹.

**Track 2 — REFER (pilot, 2–3 friendly tutors):** tutor doesn't buy — tutor becomes a paid
referrer. Tutor sends their students; parents pay Acharya's B2C plan; **tutor earns a RECURRING
₹50/month for every referred student, for as long as that student keeps paying.** Recurring (not
one-time) so the tutor stays motivated to keep students engaged = our retention engine.
- **Payout trigger:** the referred student's payment must CLEAR (never per free signup — that invites
  lead-spam). ₹0 paid out until real revenue lands.
- **Rate to finalize:** ₹50/mo flat (= 33% of a ₹150 junior, 20% of a ₹250 senior). **Consider
  ₹30 junior / ₹50 senior** for a clean ~20% either side. — Deepak to confirm.
- **Kill the conflict:** position Acharya as the tutor's tool that makes THEM look better
  ("exam-authentic home practice; you look professional, students score higher"), never a replacement.
- **Attribution:** one referral code per tutor; a simple sheet for the pilot (no system build yet).
- **Why pilot, not all-in:** referral relocates the "will you pay?" question from 1 tutor to many
  parents — unproven. Prove parent-conversion on 2–3 tutors BEFORE scaling this channel.

## THE PITCH (retargeted for a solo tutor — do NOT reuse the institute deck)

A tutor doesn't care about the 46k-question moat. They care about their Sunday. Lead with their pain:

> **"You spend every Sunday making test papers by hand. Acharya makes an exam-authentic paper in
> 10 seconds — with YOUR name and logo on it — and tells you which student is weak in which chapter.
> Free to start. Pay ₹250/month only if you're still using it after a month."**

Three hooks that land for a tutor specifically:
1. **Time saved** — paper-making goes from a Sunday to 10 seconds.
2. **Status with parents** — branded papers (self-serve `/teacher/branding`: their logo + watermark).
3. **Free-to-start** — kills the risk objection; "set it up on this tablet right now."

**Qualify in 30 sec (before you sit down):** solo/home tutor or 1-room setup · makes their own papers ·
≥10 students. **Skip** anyone who already has a question bank or only wants teaching aids (PPTs/3D).
