*Not a banner. Not to be shown up front. This is YOUR prep for one specific moment: **the owner asks
"can I sell this to other institutes/students and earn from it?"** If he doesn't raise it, don't bring

- ✅ Ideally **after** his own institute is live & happy (a few weeks) — but if he asks Sunday, you're ready.

## The model in one line

He becomes a **channel partner**: sells Acharya (his brand, *Powered by Acharya*) to other institutes/
students at **his own price**, and you share the revenue. He does the selling + support; you run the engine.

---

## The offer (LOCKED terms) — WHOLESALE model

*(Corrected 2026-08-01 from the earlier 60/40 + ₹50-floor rev-share, which was wrong: the ₹50 floor was
below our value and near cost, it cannibalized direct sales, and giving the reseller 60% over-rewarded the
side that bears no tech cost. Wholesale fixes all three.)*

> **Partner buys at a wholesale per-student rate, sells at their own price, keeps 100% of the markup.**
> We run all the tech; no revenue auditing.

| Segment | Partner pays TrigunAI (wholesale) | Sells at (list) | Partner keeps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board / Foundation | **₹80/student** | ₹120+ | ₹40+ |
| JEE / NEET / Senior | **₹150/student** | ₹250+ | ₹100+ |

Plus **₹30,000 per institute** (server + DevOps setup) and **min ₹15,000/mo**.

- **Why wholesale, not rev-share:** clean (no auditing), protects our margin regardless of the partner's
  price, and doesn't cannibalize direct sales (wholesale is ~40% below direct list; recommend the partner
  hold list so the market price isn't spoiled).
- **The finance test it passes:** TrigunAI keeps ~70–85% gross margin AND *more per student than the
  partner* (₹150 vs ₹100 on JEE) — correct, because we carry all the COGS, IP and engineering.

At scale this is pure leverage: 5 institutes × 400 students × ₹150 wholesale = **₹3,00,000/mo to you** from
a single partner — customers you never had to sell to yourself.

## Four guardrails (state these as "how the program works", not as distrust)

1. **Wholesale floor** — partner pays ≥ ₹80 (Board) / ₹150 (JEE-NEET) per student + ₹30k/institute setup. Non-negotiable; protects margin & direct pricing.
2. **"Powered by Acharya" stays on every resold instance** — mandatory. It's free marketing across his
   whole network, and it's your leverage. (He already accepted this for his own instance.)
3. **You approve who he resells to** — a bad experience he sells still carries *your* name. Quality control.
4. **A simple partner agreement** — territory, floor price, quality standard, your right to end-customer
   data, payment terms. (Draft this before any second sub-institute goes live.)

---

## The brand-risk reason to gate it (why "land him first" matters)

Every instance he resells says **Powered by Acharya**. If he sells a product he hasn't personally
validated and it disappoints, it damages *your* brand — not just his. So: he must **prove it on his own
institute first**, then earn the right to resell. Frame that to him as a feature, not a restriction:
*"Once you've seen it work for your own students, you'll sell it to others with total confidence."*

---

## The sequence (say it to yourself before the call)

1. **Sunday:** close his own institute (₹55k + ₹50/student) + lock the Chemistry pilot. **The gate.**
2. **After he's live & happy:** open THIS — the partner program — as the expansion that makes him money.
3. Reseller model = a **carrot that deepens the relationship**, never a complication to the first close.
