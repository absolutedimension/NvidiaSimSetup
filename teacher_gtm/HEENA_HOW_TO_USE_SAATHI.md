# Saathi — your work companion on WhatsApp

**For:** Heena · **How to reach it:** it messages you from the same Acharya WhatsApp number. Just reply.

---

## What Saathi is

**Not a boss and not a form.** It's a companion that knows your work: your calling list, which
institute is at what stage, what Rohan did yesterday, and what you're learning. Think of it as a
colleague who never forgets anything and is awake whenever you are.

**You never have to maintain a sheet.** You tell it what happened; it keeps the record. That record
is shared with Rohan, so he sees your bookings without you writing a report.

## The 6 things to use it for

**1 · "Aaj kisko call karun?"**
It reads your list and gives you the next ones — with phone, area, segment, and **which of the 5
scripts to use for that owner**. You never have to decide where to start.

**2 · Tell it what happened after a call — in your own words**
> *"Alliant Academy se baat hui, owner ne bola papers khud banate hain, 2 ghante lagte hain.
> Kal 5 baje demo fix hai."*

That one message updates the shared ledger. Rohan sees the booking. You write nothing else.

**3 · "Is owner ko kya bolun?"** — before or after a hard call
Ask it. It'll give you the line, the objection handling, or how to reopen a call that went badly.

**4 · Learn the product — ~20 minutes a day**
It teaches you Acharya properly: what it does, what it does NOT do, why it beats a DIY app, and how
to explain the weak-topic report over the phone without a screen. **It won't let you "pass" until
you can explain it back in your own words** — that's deliberate, and it's what makes you confident
on call number forty.

**5 · "Main kaisa kar rahi hoon?"**
It tells you honestly — what's going well and the specific things to work on. Ask any time.

**6 · Ask it anything, any time**
Product doubt, an owner's objection, a subject you're unsure we cover, how the trial works. If it
doesn't know, it says so and checks with Deepak. **It will never make up a product fact** — and
neither should you.

---

## What makes it work well

- **Tell it the truth, including the bad days.** A "nobody picked up today" is useful information,
  not a failure. It will never scold you — and an honest record is what makes its advice good.
- **Tell it the same day.** A call you remember on Friday is a call Rohan couldn't act on Tuesday.
- **Say things normally.** Hinglish, English, short messages. It's a chat, not a form.
- **Correct it when it's wrong.** If it says an institute is at visit 2 and you know it's visit 3,
  say so — it will believe you and fix the record.

---

## The one thing it can't do

**It cannot make the call for you.** It plans, teaches, remembers and reminds. The dialling is
yours.
