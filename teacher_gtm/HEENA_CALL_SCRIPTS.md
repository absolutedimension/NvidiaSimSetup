## The shape of every call

**Primary ask = a demo at their office** (Rohan comes, 10 minutes, makes a paper for their subject).

**If they resist a visit — and many will — you have a second ask that always works:**

> ## "Sir, main aapko WhatsApp par link bhej deti hoon — aap khud 2 minute mein test dekh lijiye."

That is not a consolation prize. A link costs them nothing, needs no appointment, no signup, and
they can look at it at 11pm. **Many owners will say yes to a link who would never say yes to a
visit** — and an owner who has already seen the product is a much easier visit for Rohan afterwards.

**So no call ends empty.** Every call ends with one of:

| | Outcome | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ✅ **Office demo booked** | date + time + owner's name |
| 2 | 📱 **Link sent** | **a valid WhatsApp number**, then follow up in 2 days |
| 3 | 📞 **Call back** | a specific day and time |
| 4 | ❌ **Clear no** | the real reason — log it, move on |

### ⚠️ Always confirm the WhatsApp number out loud

The number on my list is the office landline or a business number. **It may not be on WhatsApp.**

> *"Sir, main link kis number par bhejun? Yahi number WhatsApp par hai, ya koi doosra?"*

Then repeat it back digit by digit. **A link sent to a non-WhatsApp number is a lost lead you'll
think you worked.** If he prefers, take an email — but WhatsApp gets opened, email usually doesn't.

>
> **The question:** *"Aapke institute mein weekly test ke papers abhi kaun banata hai — aap khud,
> ya faculty?"*
>
> *(Let him answer. Then:)*
>
> *"Aur ek paper banane mein kitna time lag jaata hai unhe?"*

**Then the pivot — use HIS number back at him:**

> *"Sir, hamara system usi paper ko 2 minute mein bana deta hai — NEET/JEE ke asli pattern mein,

> *"Hamare Rohan aa kar aapko 10 minute mein dikha denge — aapke hi chapter ka paper banake. Kal

**If he hesitates → the link:**

> *"Koi baat nahi sir, main abhi aapko WhatsApp par link bhej deti hoon — aap khud ek test dekh
> lijiye, do minute lagega. Agar theek lage to Rohan aa jaayenge."*

# SCRIPT 4 — When the receptionist or a teacher answers
*This will be a third of your calls. Be warm — they decide whether you ever reach the owner.*

> **Open:** *"Namaste, main Heena baat kar rahi hoon TrigunAI se. Sir se baat ho sakti hai? Test
> papers ke baare mein tha."*
>
> **If "sir nahi hain":** *"Koi baat nahi. Sir kis time milte hain — subah ya shaam? Main us time
> call kar lungi."*
>
> **Then get the direct number:** *"Aur sir ka WhatsApp number mil sakta hai? Main unhe link bhej
> deti hoon, wo apne time par dekh lenge."*

**Never pitch the receptionist properly** — they can't say yes, and you'll have spent your pitch on
the wrong person. Get the time and the number, be pleasant, hang up.

**Do log it.** "Owner not available, call at 6pm" is useful. It is not a wasted call.

# SCRIPT 5 — The brush-off: "busy hoon" / "WhatsApp kar do" / "abhi nahi"
*The most common response you'll get. It is a bad moment, not a no.*

> **If "abhi busy hoon":**
> *"Bilkul sir, main disturb nahi karungi. Shaam ko 6 baje call karun ya kal subah?"*
>
> **Two options. Never "main baad mein call karungi"** — that means never.

> **If "WhatsApp par bhej do":** *This is a YES, take it.*
> *"Ji bilkul sir. Yahi number WhatsApp par hai na? Main abhi bhej rahi hoon — 2 minute ka hai,
> aap dekh lijiyega. Parso main ek call kar lungi, aap bata dijiyega kaisa laga."*
>
> **Note both things: the number, and that you'll call back in 2 days.** A link sent and never
> followed up is a lead you gave away.

> **If "humein zaroorat nahi":**
> *"Theek hai sir. Bas ek baat — abhi 14 din bilkul free hai, koi paisa nahi. Ek baar dekh lijiye,
> pasand na aaye to chhod dijiyega."*
>
> **If he still says no — accept it cleanly.** *"Koi baat nahi sir, dhanyavaad. Aapke
> jaan-pehchaan mein koi bada batch chalata hai?"* Log the real reason and move on.
> **A clean no frees your day. Chasing a real no wastes a week.**

## Rules that apply to all five

1. **Never pitch before he names a pain.** Ask, listen, then use his words.
2. **One ask per call.** Demo, or link. Not both at once.
3. **Never ask for money.** 14 days free. Price if asked — honestly. Deepak handles commercials.
4. **Never claim a subject we don't have.** Current affairs is thin — say so.
5. **Log every call the same day**, including the no's, with the real reason.
6. **Confirm the WhatsApp number out loud** before promising to send anything.
