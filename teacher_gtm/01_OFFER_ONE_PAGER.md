# The Offer — what you say to a teacher (one page)

> This is the spoken/WhatsApp version of `acharya_pamphlet/pamphlet_teacher.html`.
> Say it in the teacher's language (Hindi/English mix). Never lead with "AI" — lead
> with THEIR problem: students leaving, doubts eating evenings, competition from big apps.

## The pitch (60 seconds)

"You teach your students a few hours a week. The other 160 hours, they're alone — that's
when doubts pile up, practice doesn't happen, and weak students quietly drop out.

**Acharya is your own tutor, under your name, on WhatsApp.** Your students message it
any time — it solves doubts step by step in your subject, gives daily practice matched
to your syllabus, and revises them before exams. You see exactly who practiced, who's
stuck, and where — every week, on your phone.

Your students stay because they improve. Parents see the improvement. Your batch grows.

It's **₹4,999 a month, flat** — less than the fee of one student. First 14 days free
with 10 of your students, so you see it work before you pay anything."

## What the teacher gets (be able to demo each one)

1. **Their own WhatsApp tutor number** students message directly (the Acharya bridge).
2. **Doubt-solving in their subject** — step-by-step, Hindi or English, 24×7.
3. **Daily practice + spaced revision** matched to the syllabus/exam they teach.
4. **Weekly progress report to the teacher** — who's active, who's stuck, on what topic.
5. **Their brand on it** — Acharya introduces itself as "[Teacher name]'s tutor".

## What it is NOT (say this — it builds trust)

- NOT a replacement for the teacher. It does homework-hours support; the teacher teaches.
- NOT an app students must install — it's WhatsApp, which they already have.
- NOT a per-student fee that punishes growth — flat ₹4,999 up to 50 active students.

## The close (always the same)

"Give me 10 of your students for 14 days, free. If you don't see them practicing more,
we stop, no charge. If you do — it's ₹4,999 a month and it stays under your name."

→ Then book the onboarding slot **on the call** (don't leave it to "I'll tell you").

## Objection quick-answers

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Students already use ChatGPT" | "ChatGPT doesn't know your syllabus, doesn't give them your practice plan, and doesn't tell YOU who's falling behind. Acharya works for your class, and reports to you." |
| "₹4,999 is a lot" | "It's one student's monthly fee. If it keeps even one student from leaving, it paid for itself." |
| "My students won't use it" | "That's exactly what the free 14 days tests. If they don't use it, you pay nothing." |
| "I'm not technical" | "You do nothing technical. I set it up; your students just message a WhatsApp number." |
| "Let me think" | "Sure — can I set up the free pilot meanwhile? Thinking is easier with it running." Log the REAL objection behind it. |

## Honesty guardrails (CEO OS — do not violate)

- No "thousands of students use it" — the truth is: newly launched, first teacher partners.
- No guaranteed marks/results. Say "more practice, visible progress" — not "toppers".
- "Course completion certificate", never "industry-recognized certification".
