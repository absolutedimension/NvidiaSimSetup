Test the logo & theme before your visit

Do this once (5 minutes) so you can walk in and show any institute their own logo, colours & watermark live on a real test paper. Best of all — set up each institute's branding the night before and screenshot the branded paper to show them instantly.

1

Sign in as a teacher

Open acharya.trigunai.com/teacher → Continue with Google (or email — no password). In Institute name type the institute you're visiting (e.g. "Brilliant Classes").

2

Open Branding

In the top menu tap Branding (or the "🎨 Make it yours" card on the home screen).

3

Set it up as the institute

  • Type the institute's name (shows on every paper)
  • Pick their brand colour — tap a swatch, or paste their exact colour code
  • Upload their logo — a photo or file, under 500 KB (a PNG with clear background looks best)
  • Turn on Watermark — their logo faint behind every paper (anti-copy)
  • Watch the Live Preview on the right change · then tap Save my branding
4

Make a test

Go to My class → create a test (pick exam + chapter) → wait ~30 seconds → you get a test code.

5

See the branded paper

Open the test → Print paper. You'll see their logo at the top, their colour, the watermark behind the questions, and a small "Powered by Acharya" at the bottom. Screenshot this to show the owner.

6

See the student side

Open the share link acharya.trigunai.com/t/CODE on your phone → the student's test page shows their logo, name & colour too.

✓ Tick before any visit

  • Logo shows on the paper header
  • Brand colour on paper + student page
  • Watermark faint behind the questions
  • Institute name is correct
  • "Powered by Acharya" is there (that stays — it's the only thing that's ours)

★ Pro tips

  • Ask the owner for their logo + colour — get the logo on WhatsApp beforehand
  • Prep each institute the night before — screenshot the branded paper ready to show
  • Logo looks boxy? Ask for a transparent PNG
  • Next institute? Just open Branding again — change the name, colour & logo (or "Remove my logo")
