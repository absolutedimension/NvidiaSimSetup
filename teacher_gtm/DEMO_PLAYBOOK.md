# Acharya Demo Playbook — how Rohan demos in the room (3 minutes, unforgettable)

> **The one principle:** people believe what they **do**, not what they watch. So the whole demo is
> *"hand them the phone."* You talk less; the owner's fingers on WhatsApp, on THEIR subject, is the sale.
> A polished video is the *weakest* demo — use it only when signal drops. Companion: `ROHAN_FIELD_PITCH.md`
> (the pitch), `FIELD_RESEARCH_GUIDE.md` (discovery first — you demo the pain they just named).

## Before you walk in (30-sec pre-check — never let a demo fail on friction)
- ✅ The **right demo tenant open** on your phone for their subject (NEET Bio / JEE Physics / Class 10

## THE 3-MINUTE FLOW (5 steps — run it the same way every time)

**Step 1 · Recall their pain (10 sec).** Tie the demo to what THEY just told you.
> *"Sir, aapne abhi bताya — raat ko students ke doubts ka jawab nahi milता. Ek minute, main dिखाता hoon."*

**Step 2 · Hand them the phone — THEY ask (60 sec).** This is the whole demo. Don't type it yourself.
> *"Aap khud type kijiye — jo doubt aapke students aksar poochte हैं, apne subject se."*
> The owner types a real doubt → Acharya answers **live, in Hindi, step-by-step, in a teaching style.**
> They're watching *their own subject* get taught. Stay quiet and let it land.

**Step 3 · The WOW — the confidently-wrong catch (30 sec).** This is your differentiator. Set it up:
> *"Ab ek cheez dिखाता hoon jo koi aur nahi karta. Aap jaan-boojh kar GALAT jawab dijiye — poore
> confidence se, jaise ek over-confident student deta hai."*
> The owner gives a confident wrong answer → Acharya **catches it**: *"aapko laga sahi hai, par yahaan
> gap hai…"* and repairs it. Then land the line:
> *"Ye normal AI nahi hai jo bas jawab de deta hai. Ye bachche ko galat samajhne nahi deta — deeply
> samajhata hai. Yehi farq hai."*

**Step 4 · Leave it running — the demo BECOMES the pilot (20 sec).** Don't end it. Don't ask "kaisा lagा?"
> *"Chaliye, aaj hi aapke ek batch pe free laga dete हैं — aapke naam se. Students use karein, aap khud
> dekhिye. Setup main karता hoon."*
> Capture: subject to go live on, batch size, WhatsApp opt-in. The demo never "ends" → no "let me think."

## The demo variations (match the owner)

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Busy owner ("2 minute hai")** | Skip to Step 2 + 3 only. One doubt + the confidently-wrong catch. 90 seconds. |
| **Skeptic ("AI se padhai nahi hoti")** | Lead with the **ChatGPT vs Acharya** contrast: type the same doubt in both. ChatGPT = generic English answer-dump; Acharya = Hindi, their style, step-by-step, their brand. The difference sells itself. |
| **No signal / glitch** | Straight to the **60-sec fallback video**, then: *"main aapko ek link deta hoon, aap baad mein khud try kijiye"* (QR leave-behind). Never fumble a live demo on bad network. |
| **Wants to feel the teaching** | The **"become the weak student"** role-play: *"aap ek kamzor student ban jaiye"* — Acharya teaches THEM (guess-first, explains from their attempt). They feel the method. |
| **Owner won't touch the phone** | You type, but narrate it as THEIR student: *"maan lijiye aapka student ye poochta hai raat ko…"* Still on their subject. |

---

## What NOT to do (these kill demos)
- ❌ **Don't type it yourself if they'll touch the phone.** Their fingers = belief. Yours = a pitch.
- ❌ **Don't demo a generic subject.** Always THEIR exam/subject, or *"ye mere kaam ka nahi."*
- ❌ **Don't feature-tour.** Demo the ONE pain they named. The rest is noise.
- ❌ **Don't demo anything that isn't live.** If they ask for something we don't have (test-maker,
  parent-updates), say *"abhi ye ban raha hai"* and **log it** — never mock a fake screen.
- ❌ **Don't over-talk.** After you hand them the phone, shut up. Silence + their own experience = the close.
- ❌ **Don't beg for a verdict.** Move straight to "chaliye ek batch pe laga dete हैं" — assume the pilot.

## ⚙️ How the tenant actually behaves — demo it RIGHT
Acharya is a **structured tutor, not a doubt-dump** (this is the selling point vs ChatGPT). So:
- Owner types a doubt → Acharya gives a **quick answer, then teaches the concept step-by-step** with a
  hook + analogy. It won't just spit a paragraph like Google — **frame that as the value**: *"ye ChatGPT
  nahi hai jo answer phenk deta hai — ye aapke bachche ko samajhata hai, foundation se."*
- **The star move = the confidently-wrong catch (Step 3).** After Acharya asks a check question, tell the
  owner to answer it *confidently but wrong* → Acharya catches it. This works on ANY concept and is the
  one thing no free AI does. **Lead the demo with this**, not with free doubt-asking.
- Keep the **60-sec fallback video** on Rohan's phone for no-signal.
