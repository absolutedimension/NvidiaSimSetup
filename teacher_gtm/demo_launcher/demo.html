🎯 THE KILLER FEATURE — Rohan, ise dhyaan se samajh

Acharya khud pehchaan leta hai kaun student weak hai — aur uske liye kya karna hai woh bhi BATA deta hai. Teacher sirf ek tap kare.

Yeh sabse bada kyun hai: ek teacher ke paas 50 students hain. Woh akela kaise track kare — kaun kis topic mein weak hai, kaun improve kar raha, kaun peeche gir raha? Insaani taur pe impossible hai. Acharya yeh automatically karta hai:

  1. Weak & slipping students khud flag karta hai — "in 5 ko madad chahiye, ye 3 gayab ho rahe हैं."
  2. Har ek ka exact weak topic pinpoint — "Sneha — Current Electricity kaccha hai."
  3. Suggested test khud recommend karta hai + teacher sirf "Do it" ek tap kare → test assign, ho gaya. Banana kuch nahi.

Iska matlab: 50 students, par har ek ko personalized next step — jo ek insaan akele kabhi de hi nahi sakta. Teacher ka ghante ka kaam ek tap mein. Yehi woh feature hai jispe owner bolega "haan, mujhe yeh chahiye."

"Aapko sirf padhaana hai. Kaun weak hai, kya assign karna hai — Acharya khud bata deta hai, ek tap mein ho jaata hai. Aapko sochna bhi nahi padta."

How to demo — 30 seconds

  1. Tap their subject → hand them the phone. They take a live adaptive test.
  2. The money move: tell them to answer Q1 jaan-boojh kar GALAT → watch it drop to basics.
  3. Open the Teacher Dashboard → → weak student pe tap → dikhao Acharya khud suggestion deta hai → "Do it" dabao. Yahi clincher hai.

🌐 English or हिन्दी — dono

Har test ke upar language toggle hai. Teacher ki language mein dikhाओ — English medium ho ya Hindi medium, ek tap mein switch. Bolo: "Aapke students jis bhi language mein comfortable हैं — English ya हिन्दी — Acharya usi mein test leta hai."
