🪔 Acharya — Assessment Demo Guide

What to show, exactly what to say, and how to run the demo so the teacher says yes.

What Acharya is (in one line): an AI assessment engine. It tests your students — topic by topic, adaptively — grades them instantly, and shows the teacher who is weak where on a dashboard. You teach; Acharya tests & tracks. It doesn't replace the teacher — it takes off the one job teachers hate and never have time for: making tests, checking them, and tracking every student. Frame it that way every time.

The #1 rule of a good demo

Hand them the phone. Let them take the test themselves. People believe what they experience, not what they watch. You talk 20%; their fingers on the screen do the other 80%. Say: "Aap khud ek student ban ke test dijiye — jaise aapka bachcha dega."

Why assessment (not teaching)

Doubt-solving is free now — ChatGPT does it. That's not what an institute lacks or will pay for. What they lack is assessment: nobody has time to test every student on every topic, grade it, and track who's weak where. That's the real gap — and it's the one thing that never threatens the teacher. Lead with it.

What to test — and exactly how

1️⃣ A live adaptive test — just tap, in Hindi

Tap the subject → hand them the phone. Your student gets an adaptive test — MCQ, True/False, match-the-following, numerical, and one written answer — all by simple taps, one question per screen, in Hindi.

Test it: open a subject and just tap through 2–3 questions. Notice the instant green/red feedback + a one-line explanation on every answer. It feels like a real test, not a chat.

⭐ 2️⃣ It ADAPTS when the student is stuck — THE money move

When a student gets a question wrong, Acharya drops to an easier question and reminds the basics — then builds back up. A human teacher can't do this for 50 students at once. Show it every single demo.

Test it: tell the owner to answer the first question WRONG on purpose. Watch the test become easier + show a hint that reminds the basic — then continue.

Then say: "Dekha? Bachcha atak gaya toh yeh chhodta nahi — level neeche laake basics pakke karta hai, phir aage. Ek teacher 50 bacchon ke saath yeh kar hi nahi sakta."

3️⃣ It grades instantly + finds each student's weak spots

At the end, a clean score card shows which topics are solid and which are weak — per student. No checking papers, no manual tracking.

Test it: finish the 5-question test and show the owner the weak-spot card at the end (e.g. "Latent Heat — weak"). Say: "Ye khud jaanch leta hai — aapko paper check nahi karna."

🎯 4️⃣ The MAGIC dashboard — it tells the teacher WHAT TO DO

This is the feature that makes an owner say "I need this." Every test feeds a dashboard that doesn't just show data — it does the teacher's thinking. Open the live dashboard →

What the teacher sees, at a glance:

  • Class at a glance — one colour bar: how many students are Strong / Average / Need-help, and a heat-map of which topics the batch is weak on (so they know what to teach next).
  • Per student, a mini SWOT — 💪 Strong areas (green) vs ⚠️ Weak areas (red), in one look.
  • A trend line — is the student improving ↑ or dropping ↓? (Direction matters more than today's mark.)
  • 🎯 A suggested ACTION for each student — e.g. "Weak in Current Electricity — assign a basics-first drill; likely to jump to 70%." One tap on "Do it →" and it's done.
Say it: "Yeh sirf data nahi dikhata — har bachche ke liye BATATA hai ki kya karna hai. Kaun weak hai, kaun improve kar raha hai, aur uske liye kya assign karein — ek tap mein. Aapko sochna nahi padta."

📊 How to demo the magic dashboard (60 sec)

  1. Open it on your phone (launcher → "Teacher dashboard") and hand it over — or scroll it together.
  2. Point at the colour bar: "Ek nazar mein — 8 strong, 11 average, 5 ko madad chahiye."
  3. Tap the heat-map: "Poora batch Wave Optics par weak hai — ek tap, sabko test assign."
  4. Tap a weak student (Sneha) → show 💪Strong / ⚠️Weak + the ↑ improving trend.
  5. The clincher — the suggestion: "Dekho — Acharya khud bata raha hai kya karna hai. 'Do it' dabao, ho gaya." Tap Do it →.

5️⃣ A whole test-SERIES, under the teacher's brand

Not one test — a continuous, topic-wise test series that runs on autopilot, carrying the institute's name. It makes the institute look the most advanced in the area. (These demo subjects are generic; Deepak brands + loads the teacher's own syllabus for a pilot.)

The 3-minute in-room flow

  1. Recall their pain: "Aap har student ka, har topic ka test alag se le paate हैं? Banana, jaanchna, track karna — time hi nahi milta na."
  2. Tap their subject → hand them the phone: "Aap khud student ban ke test dijiye." Let them tap through it.
  3. The money move: "pehla sawaal jaan-boojh kar galat kijiye" → watch it adapt to basics. Do this every time.
  4. Show the weak-spot card + the dashboard line (feature 4).
  5. Leave it running = the pilot: "Ek batch ka test-series 2 hafte free laga dete हैं — aapke naam se, aapko kuch banana nahi." Assume the yes.

The value line (say it out loud)

"Ek human assistant test banaye-jaanche toh ₹15,000/mo, aur woh raat ko so jaata hai. Yeh ₹4,999 — har student ki fees se bhi kam — aur chौबीs ghante, har bacche ka."

If the owner is…

  • Busy ("2 min hai"): just the adaptive money move — one wrong answer, watch it drop to basics. 90 seconds.
  • "Main khud test leta hoon": "Aap lete हैं — par har student ka, har topic ka, roz? Aur kaun kahan weak hai, individually? Yeh woh kaam hai jo 50 bacchon ke saath mushkil hai. Aap padhaao, test aur tracking yeh kare."
  • Skeptic ("AI test kaise lega"): don't argue — hand the phone. They take the test, see the grading + weak-spots. It sells itself.
  • "Questions galat honge?": "Abhi verified, curated bank hai; aapke syllabus pe aap khud review kar sakte हैं."
  • No signal: play the 60-sec video, then share the demo link so they take a test later.

4 rules — never break

  • • Hand them the phone — unhe test dene do, aap kam bolo.
  • • Unke subject pe demo karo, generic nahi.
  • • Kabhi marks/results promise mat karo; "thousands use it" mat bolo — hum naye हैं, first partners dhoondh rahe हैं.
  • • Paisa kabhi mat lo — sirf official link pe.

Be honest — it protects the sale

  • • The demo has a short curated set (~5 questions per subject) — real and correct, but a taste. Don't call it the full test-series.
  • • The teacher dashboard is rolling out — describe it, don't show a fake one. In a pilot, Deepak hands the teacher a real weak-spot report by hand.
  • • If a real need comes up that Acharya doesn't do yet, don't fake it — note it and tell Deepak. That's a win too.

Remember: you're not selling — you're taking a burden off them. Aap padhaao; testing & tracking Acharya sambhaale.
