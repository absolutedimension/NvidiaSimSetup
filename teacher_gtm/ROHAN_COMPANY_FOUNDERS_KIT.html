Who We Are — Company & Founders

When an institute owner asks "Which company is this? Who runs it?" — open this and tell them directly. Everything here is true; say it plainly, never exaggerate.
Our vision: use agentic AI to solve the real problems that coaching institutes face — an AI layer for education.
TrigunAI Innovations Pvt Ltd does not come to sell a product. We first understand the real problem an institute has, then solve it with AI. Every institute's problem can be different — for some it's making test papers, for some attendance, for some fee follow-up, for some tracking each student. We are building an agentic AI system for all of these. We are not a "ChatGPT wrapper" — we build our own technology; our VR app is already live on the Meta Quest store (we ship real products, we don't just talk).
Our first hypothesis — what we are testing right now
Test / exam-paper generation + tracking each student's weak topics. This is our first solution — Acharya. We are checking it with institutes: "Do you face this problem?" If yes → we set it up free with Acharya and show you. And if your bigger problem is something else — attendance, fees, parent updates — tell us; that could become our next AI solution.
🔎 Rohan's real job = research, not selling. Goal of every visit: clearly understand one real problem the owner has. Ask — "How do you make test papers today? Is that a pain? Apart from that, where does most of your time / trouble go?" If Acharya fits that pain → free pilot. If it doesn't → log that problem; it may be our next solution.
