## 2. HOW a call goes (the flow)

Keep it natural. The whole thing is ~8–12 minutes. **Talk 30%, listen 70%.**

### Step 1 — Opening (30 seconds)
> "Namaste [name] ji — main Priyanshu bol raha hoon, TrigunAI se. Hum teachers ke liye ek
> AI tutor banate hain jo aapke *apne naam se* WhatsApp pe chalta hai. Kuch bechne se pehle
> main samajhna chahta hoon ki aapki classes kaise chalti hain — bas 2 minute, kuch sawaal
> puch sakta hoon?"

If they're busy: *"Koi baat nahi, main [time] pe call kar loon?"* — book a callback, note it.

### Step 2 — Discovery (their turn — ask, then LISTEN, write down what they say)
Ask these one at a time. The goal is to find *the problem that makes them talk fast*:

**Their business:**
1. "Abhi kitne students hain? Kaun si class / exam? Monthly fees kitni hai?"
2. "Naye students kaise aate hain? Pichhla student kyun chhod ke gaya?"

**Their pain (listen for the one that stings):**
3. "Jab kisi student ko raat 9 baje doubt aata hai — tab kya hota hai?"
4. "Aapki shaam ka kitna time doubt messages mein jaata hai?"
5. "Aapko kaise pata chalta hai ki koi student chupchaap peeche chhoot raha hai — test result se pehle?"
6. "Kya kabhi students bade apps (Physics Wallah, BYJU'S type) ki wajah se gaye hain?"

**Money:**
7. "Agar mahine mein 2 extra students tik jaayein, toh aapke liye woh kitne ka fayda hai?"
   *(Their answer prices it for them: 2 students' fees vs our ₹4,999.)*

### Step 3 — Only pitch if they qualify (3 of these 4 must be true)
- [ ] Has **10+ paying students** right now
- [ ] Teaches an **exam-prep subject** (board / NEET / govt exam)
- [ ] Named a **real pain** in the questions above (doubts / retention / competition)
- [ ] **They own the money decision** (it's their tuition)

**If they DON'T qualify:** thank them, ask *"aap kisi ko jaante hain jo bada batch chalata hai?"*
(get a referral name+number), note it, move on. Don't pitch the wrong person.

### Step 4 — The pitch (only after discovery — see Section 3)
Repeat their pain back first: *"Aapne kaha [their exact words]… iska seedha hal ye hai…"*

### Step 5 — The close + opt-in
> "10 students dijiye, 14 din, bilkul free. Kaam kare toh ₹4,999 mahina, na kare toh band —
> koi charge nahi. Main aapko WhatsApp pe details aur ek demo bhej doon?"

- If **YES to pilot** → book onboarding time now: *"Kal shaam 6 baje set kar dein?"*
- If **"WhatsApp me"** → that's the opt-in. Now (and only now) send the WhatsApp message (Section 5).
- **Log the call** (Section 6) — every time.
