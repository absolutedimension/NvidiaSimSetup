## Your call, in four steps

**1 · Open in 20 seconds** — before he decides you're a telemarketer.

> *"Namaste sir, main Heena baat kar rahi hoon TrigunAI se. Aap coaching chalate hain na — main
> sirf 2 minute lungi. Aapke institute mein test papers kaun banata hai abhi?"*

**2 · Make him name the pain** — don't pitch yet. Ask, then listen:

- *"Test papers abhi kaun banata hai, aur ek paper mein kitna time lag jaata hai?"*
- *"Kitni baar test hota hai — kabhi paper ready na hone se test skip hua hai?"*
- *"Test ke baad kaise pata chalta hai ki poora batch kis chapter mein kamzor hai?"*

Whichever makes him talk fast is the pain. **Write down his exact words** — Rohan opens with them.

**3 · Qualify — 3 of these 4 must be true before you book:**

- [ ] 10+ students
- [ ] Teaches an exam-prep subject we cover (see table above)
- [ ] Named a real pain about test papers
- [ ] **You are talking to the person who decides**

**Not qualified → do NOT book.** Thank him, ask *"aapke jaan-pehchaan mein koi bada batch chalata
hai?"*, log the reason, move on. **Protecting Rohan's day IS your job.** A booking that shouldn't
have been made costs him half a day, and costs you his trust in this whole list.

**4 · Book a specific slot**

> *"Sir, hamare Rohan aapke paas aa kar 10 minute mein dikha denge — aapke hi subject ka paper
> banake. Kal shaam 5 baje theek rahega, ya parso subah?"*

**Two options, never "kab aayein?"** An open question gets "baad mein bata denge."

## Every call ends in exactly one of three ways

| Outcome | What you write |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Booked** | date + time + owner's name + the pain in his words |
| ❌ **Not interested** | the real reason — this is useful, not a failure |
| 📞 **Call back** | a **specific** day and time — never "baad mein" |

**Nothing vague.** *"Achhi baat hui"* with nothing agreed is a call that moved nothing.

## Money — there is no money conversation

It starts on a **14-day FREE TRIAL**. Your ask is always *"trial shuru karke dekh lijiye"* — never
*"kitna doge."*

**If he asks the price, tell him honestly.** Dodging it makes you sound like you're hiding
something and kills trust faster than any number. Nobody is paying today, so being straight costs
nothing. Then:

> *"Sir, abhi 14 din bilkul free hai. Pehle chala ke dekh lijiye. Uske baad Deepak sir aapse baat
> kar lenge."*

**You never ask for money, negotiate, or give a discount.** That is Deepak's conversation, after
the trial.

## A realistic day

**40–50 dials → ~15 conversations → 2–4 booked demos.** That is a good day, not a bad one.

Most calls will not reach the owner. That is normal and is not a reflection on you. **The list is
long on purpose.**

Tell Saathi how each call went — it keeps the shared ledger so Rohan sees your bookings without you
writing a separate report, and so nobody calls an institute he visited that morning.
