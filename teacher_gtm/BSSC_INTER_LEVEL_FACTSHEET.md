## 2. The post

Almost all vacancies are **निम्नवर्गीय लिपिक (Lower Division Clerk)** and equivalent inter-level posts
across ~65 Bihar government departments.

| | |
|---|---|
| Pay | **Level-2** |
| Minimum education | **इंटरमीडिएट (10+2) या समकक्ष** |
| Technical requirement | Computer knowledge — Hindi word-processing / typing on computer; several posts also require English typing and computer operation |
| Age (as on **01.08.2025**) | Minimum **18** |

**Maximum age** (GAD resolution 294, 07.01.2016):

| Category | Max age |
|---|---|
| अनारक्षित (पुरुष) | 37 |
| पिछड़ा वर्ग एवं अत्यंत पिछड़ा वर्ग (पुरुष एवं महिला) | 40 |
| अनारक्षित (महिला) | 40 |
| अनुसूचित जाति / जनजाति (पुरुष एवं महिला) | 42 |
| दिव्यांग (सभी कोटि) | +10 years over the above |

Date of birth is taken from the **Matriculation certificate**.

**Fee: ₹100 for every candidate** (GAD resolution 15568, 21.08.2025) — processing charges and service
tax extra, paid online. Note this is unusually flat: there is no separate higher fee for general
candidates in this advertisement.

## 3. Exam pattern — PRELIMS (this is what a practice paper must match)

| | |
|---|---|
| Nature | वस्तुनिष्ठ (objective, multiple choice) |
| **Total questions** | **150** |
| **Marks per correct answer** | **+4** → total **600 marks** |
| **Negative marking** | **−1 per wrong answer** |
| **Duration** | **2 hours 15 minutes** |
| Options | 4 |
| Language | Hindi **and** English |
| **Conflict rule** | *If the Hindi and English versions of a question differ, **the English question prevails***. |

**Three sections, one paper:**
1. **खंड (क) सामान्य अध्ययन** — General Studies
2. **खंड (ख) सामान्य विज्ञान एवं गणित** — General Science & Mathematics
3. **खंड (ग) मानसिक क्षमता जाँच** — Comprehension / Logic / Reasoning / Mental Ability

> The advertisement does **not** state a fixed 50/50/50 split — it names the three sections without
> per-section counts. A 50/50/50 paper is a reasonable teaching assumption, not an official rule.

### ⭐ The detail most people miss: **the prelim is an OPEN-BOOK exam**

Under **clause 12 of the BSSC Exam Conduct Rules 2010 (परीक्षा संचालन नियमावली–2010)**, the
preliminary exam is conducted **पुस्तक सहित** — candidates may carry books into the hall:

- **Exactly three books**, one per section: (i) सामान्य अध्ययन, (ii) गणित, (iii) सामान्य विज्ञान.
- **Only TEXTBOOKS of NCERT / B.S.E.B. / I.C.S.E. and other recognised boards.**
- **Banned:** guides, photocopies of books, handwritten paper, notes, electronic devices.
- The candidate **must write their own name and roll number** on each book.
- **Exchanging books during the exam is completely prohibited.**
- If anything **other than the name and roll number** is found written in the book — or any material
  beyond the permitted textbooks is carried in — **candidature is cancelled** and legal action follows.

This single rule should shape how an institute teaches this exam: speed of *locating* a fact in a
textbook matters as much as recall, and the sections where books help least (Reasoning, and applied
Maths under time pressure) are where marks are actually won.

### Selection process
- A **prelim is held only if more than 40,000 applications** are received (Rules 2010). With 25,311
  vacancies and a state-wide inter-level pool, a prelim is effectively certain.
- The prelim **may run in multiple shifts**, in which case **normalisation (समानीकरण)** is applied.
- The prelim shortlists **5× the category-wise vacancies** for the Mains.
- **Mains is advertised separately**, and post preferences are collected at that stage.
- **Prelim is screening only.**

### Qualifying marks (GAD resolution 2374, 16.07.2007)
| Category | Minimum |
|---|---|
| सामान्य वर्ग | **40%** |
| पिछड़ा वर्ग | **36.5%** |
| अत्यंत पिछड़ा वर्ग | **34%** |
| अनुसूचित जाति / जनजाति | **32%** |
| महिला (सभी वर्ग) | **32%** |
| दिव्यांग (सभी वर्ग) | **32%** |

### Tie-breaking
1. Higher marks
2. Then **older candidate ranked higher**
3. Then alphabetical order of name (A→Z)

## 4. Official syllabus (verbatim structure from the advertisement)

### खंड (क) — सामान्य अध्ययन
Tests general awareness of the environment around the candidate and its application to society;
questions on current events and on everyday observation and scientific outlook that any educated
person is expected to have. **Special emphasis on Bihar, India and its neighbouring countries.**

**(i) सम-सामयिक विषय (Current Affairs)** — वैज्ञानिक प्रगति · राष्ट्रीय/अंतर्राष्ट्रीय पुरस्कार · भारतीय भाषाएँ ·
पुस्तक · लिपि · राजधानी · मुद्रा · खेल-खिलाड़ी · महत्वपूर्ण घटनाएँ

**(ii) भारत और उसके पड़ोसी देश** — पड़ोसी देशों का इतिहास · भारत का इतिहास · संस्कृति · भूगोल · आर्थिक परिदृश्य ·
स्वतंत्रता आन्दोलन · भारतीय कृषि तथा प्राकृतिक संसाधनों की प्रमुख विशेषताएँ · भारत का संविधान एवं राज्य व्यवस्था ·
देश की राजनीतिक प्रणाली · पंचायती राज · सामुदायिक विकास · पंचवर्षीय योजना · **राष्ट्रीय आन्दोलन में बिहार का योगदान**

### खंड (ख) — सामान्य विज्ञान एवं गणित
**Explicitly pegged at मैट्रिक (Class 10) level.**

**(i) सामान्य विज्ञान** — भौतिक शास्त्र · रसायन शास्त्र · जीव विज्ञान · भूगोल

**(ii) गणित** — संख्या पद्धति · पूर्ण संख्याओं का अभिकलन · दशमलव और भिन्न · संख्याओं के बीच परस्पर संबंध ·
मूलभूत अंक गणितीय संक्रियाएँ · प्रतिशत · अनुपात तथा समानुपात · औसत · ब्याज · लाभ और हानि

*Note what is NOT there: no algebra, no trigonometry, no geometry/mensuration named. This is an
arithmetic-heavy syllabus.*

### खंड (ग) — मानसिक क्षमता जाँच
Both **शाब्दिक (verbal) and गैर-शाब्दिक (non-verbal)** questions:

सादृश्य (analogy) · समानता एवं भिन्नता · स्थान कल्पना (spatial) · समस्या समाधान · विश्लेषण · दृश्य स्मृति ·
विभेद · अवलोकन · संबंध अवधारणा · अंक गणितीय तर्कशक्ति · अंक गणितीय संख्या श्रृंखला · **कूट लेखन एवं कूट व्याख्या**
(coding–decoding)

## 5. Reservation rules worth knowing

- **Women — 35% horizontal**, and per GAD 12575 (09.07.2025) this is available **only to women who
  are original residents (मूल निवासी) of Bihar**, in both reserved and unreserved categories.
- **Freedom fighters' grandchildren — 2% horizontal.** Requires the prescribed certificate from the
  District Magistrate; **a freedom fighter's successor identity card is NOT accepted.**
- **EWS** — income & asset certificate in the prescribed format; valid **one year** from issue.
  Out-of-state candidates cannot claim it. For a married woman the certificate is issued from her
  husband's residence, but she must also produce her father's-residence certificate proving Bihar
  domicile.
- **Transgender / third gender** candidates receive **पिछड़ा वर्ग (BC)** reservation benefit.
- **PwD** — 40%+ benchmark disability; scribe (श्रुतिलेखक) must be requested in the online form.
- **Contract employees** — up to **5 marks per year of satisfactory service, max 25 marks**, and only
  for the *same post* they served on. The benefit applies at **both prelim and mains** stages
  (GAD letter 12956, 16.08.2024).

## 6. What this means for a practice paper

The paper we build matches the official prelim scheme exactly:

| Official rule | Our paper |
|---|---|
| 150 questions | ✅ |
| +4 / −1, 600 marks | ✅ |
| 2 hours 15 minutes | ✅ |
| 4 options | ✅ |
| Three sections: GS / Science+Maths / Reasoning | ✅ |
| Bilingual, English authoritative on conflict | ✅ (bilingual build) |

**What we cannot claim:** that any question is a "2nd Inter Level previous year question". The exam
has not happened. Our real questions come from *other* BSSC exams at the same or adjacent level —
LDC/Welfare Organiser (02/25), Clerk Group D→C (0111), Office Attendant (02/22), 10th and 8th Level
(2024) — and each source advertisement number can be shown to the institute.

**Two syllabus-driven corrections to how we generate:**
1. **Maths should be arithmetic-only** — the official syllabus names number system, fractions,
   percentage, ratio, average, interest, profit & loss. It does **not** name algebra, trigonometry or
   mensuration. Some questions in our current maths bank (from Advt 0111, a clerk exam) include AP,
   polynomials and circle geometry, which are **above this syllabus**.
2. **Science is Class-10 level and includes भूगोल (geography)** under General Science — unusual, and
   worth teaching as such.

## 8. Open questions — do NOT guess these in front of an institute

1. **When is the prelim?** Unknown. No date announced as of 20.08.2026, six months after applications
   closed. Watch the notice board.
2. **Will it be computer-based?** On 29.07.2026 the commission floated an RFP for an agency to conduct
   **end-to-end computer-based tests (CBTs)** for its recruitment exams. That is procurement, not a
   decision about this exam — but it is a real signal worth watching.
3. **Exact per-section question split.** The advertisement names three sections but no per-section
   counts. 50/50/50 is an assumption.
