> Owner: Deepak (solo) · Created 2026-06-18 · 30 days to launch
> Budget: ₹5–15k (retargeting/boost only) · Warm base: personal network · Time: ~20 hrs/wk
> Goal: **5–10 PAID students** in the VR/MR live cohort by July 18. Paid = cleared payment, not a verbal yes.

---

## 0. The honest frame (read first)

You do **not** have a reach problem. You have **18 course modules + 7 episodes** already public on
two YouTube channels (EN+HI). What you lack is the **bottom of the funnel**: a capture page, a
payment link, and a conversion event. Fix that first or all reach leaks.

Two jobs, do not confuse them:
- **Job A — get the first 5–10 paid by July 18.** Engine = **warm 1:1 outreach + a free live
  masterclass that ends in a cohort pitch.** Personal-network-only means this is where the money is.
- **Job B — build the reach + email list** so cohort #2 isn't network-dependent. Engine = the
  content pipeline. This compounds; it does not pay rent by July 18.

**Rule:** every piece of content points to ONE place — the waitlist/registration page. CTAs aim at
buyers (Personas B = future-proofer, C = crossover pro, D = builder), not free watchers (A).

conversion event. **The real bottleneck is CLASS REQUESTS = 0 — nobody's asked for the free class yet.**

## 3. Content strategy — 4 pillars, mapped to your pipeline

Your video + audio pipeline is the force multiplier. Each pillar specifies the format and which
pipeline produces it. All content ends with the same CTA: **"Free cohort waitlist → link in bio/desc."**

| Pillar | Angle | Why it converts | Format → pipeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Proof-of-shipping** | "I have a VR app **live on the Meta Quest store** (+ 2–3 in testing). Here's exactly how I shipped it." | Your strongest asset — and it proves the exact course promise (zero → published on Quest). Buyers pay for "I did this" over "here's the docs." Lead with the LIVE app; call the others "in testing," never "live." | LinkedIn text+clip; YouTube long-form; Shorts (build moments) — **video pipeline** |
| **2. The outcome** | What the student walks out able to BUILD (a shipped VR app), not what they'll "learn." | Sells transformation, not information. Aims at B/C/D. | Reels/Shorts demos; carousel; masterclass — **video pipeline** |
| **3. AI-coding-partner** | "Build VR without being a hardcore coder — AI writes the Unity/C#, you direct it." | The unique differentiator + lowers the fear barrier for non-IT (Persona C). | Short screen-demos; LinkedIn how-to — **video pipeline** |
| **4. Universal-Mind hook** | The free episodes (mind/AI). TOFU reach magnet. | Persona A reach → funnel into B/D. Brand glue. | Episodes (done); Shorts cutdowns — **video + music pipeline** |

**Production rules for one person:**
- **Batch.** One pipeline session → a week of Shorts. Don't produce daily.
- **Repurpose ruthlessly.** Every long-form → 3–5 Shorts → LinkedIn clips → a podcast-style audio cut (music pipeline beds). One idea, six surfaces.
- **Bilingual where free:** EN+HI on episodes/Shorts (you already do this) widens reach at near-zero marginal cost.
- **Certificate is NOT a selling point yet** — lead with proof-of-shipping + outcome. No "industry-recognized." No healing/hype framing.

## 5. Metrics — track weekly, one number rules

All five live on the admin dashboard already — read them there weekly.

| Metric (dashboard field) | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **PAID (LIVE COHORT)** | **THE GATE** | Cleared payments. Nothing else counts as validation. Now 0. |
| **CLASS REQUESTS** | Leading #1 | The pre-launch validation signal. Now 0 — this is the first number to move. Zero by Jul 6 = topic/CTA not landing. |
| Free-class attendance | Leading #2 | Predicts closes; who actually shows > who requested. |
| TOTAL USERS / list size | Compounding | The asset that makes cohort #2 not network-dependent. Strip test accounts when reading it (real ≈ 2 today). |
| Cost per class-request (on the ₹5–15k) | Efficiency | Tells you if paid is worth scaling later. |

Vanity metrics to **ignore for the gate**: views, likes, subscriber count. Watchers ≠ buyers.
